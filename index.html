A few thoughts on $GOOG $GOOGL

1. Google is in its second-largest drawdown of the past 15 years, and it happened fast.

2. Over those 15 years, the stock went through one ~42% drawdown, three ~30% drawdowns, and four ~20% drawdowns, and still compounded at ~22% annually.

3. Cloud revenue grew 103% YoY last quarter. Cloud was 11% of total revenue in Q3 2023 and 24% in Q3 2026, and in my view that share keeps growing.

4. Search grew 12% YoY. The sequential deceleration, paired with the risk of AI cannibalizing Search, seems to be what has the bears loudest right now. But that 12% came on top of a 14.5% comp, a ~13.3% CAGR from Q3 2024 to Q3 2026.

With all that being said, let me ask you a simple question:

When do you think buyers of Google stock have historically made the most outsized returns?

Was it during periods of strength and bullish sentiment, or was it usually during periods of weakness and negative sentiment? I think you already know the answer, but I still must ask it to bring some reason to the conversation...

I'm not dismissing the Search slowdown. But I believe today's extreme negative sentiment may have created a gap between the price and the business's intrinsic value.

Those bearish on Google today are likely the same ones who were bearish during peak Search fears over the last few years. Ironically, many of them likely turned bullish closer to the stock's peak(s), once the bearish Search sentiment subsided and the skies seemed clear. In both cases, they agreed with the crowd, even though it has often paid to be a contrarian when investing in incredible businesses.

Which side are you on?

5. Yes, on the call, Google noted: 'It's worth noting that the strong growth in AI mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI mode.'

But do you really think Google will just accept that, with no plan to monetize its "AI installed base?" As @edantaylor stated:

'Ads in AI Mode are expanding to new countries worldwide. 🚀 As Search becomes more conversational, our research shows that people are comfortable with ads in AI Mode and find them highly relevant. We're excited to expand this high-value experience to more countries in Q4.'

Many bears seem to have written Google off (again) without considering its ability to monetize that installed base.

Finally, on the technical side, Google's RSI is near 32, close to its lowest of the past decade, and the stock is sitting right on its April 2025 anchored VWAP ($276). Tech's overall earnings multiple is also near its lowest since the 2022 lows.

And as Elon Musk suggested on March 19, 2026: 'Google will win the AI race in the West, China on Earth and SpaceX in space. Worth noting, coming from one of the greatest entrepreneurs and innovators of our time.

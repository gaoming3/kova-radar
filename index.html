Everyone talks about selling $XRP at $5, $10, $100 or even $1,000.

What if the bigger future is earning from XRP without constantly selling the core position?

That is the part of Asheesh Birla’s Evernorth strategy I think people need to understand.

Grab a coffee. This one deserves a proper read.

Evernorth is building around a simple idea: hold a large XRP treasury, put portions of it to work, generate returns, retain those returns and try to grow the amount of XRP represented by each XRPN share.

The company entered public markets with roughly 473M XRP.

Then the pieces started moving quickly.

October 9: Evernorth completed its public-market transaction.

October 12: Hugo Jeandesboz became CIO after working at Coinbase Asset Management, HSBC and Société Générale.

October 14: Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo were named as Evernorth’s first institutional execution partners, with initial strategies expected to begin in Q4 2026.

Now think about the difference between simply holding XRP and making the asset productive.

Take an illustrative holder with 1M XRP.

Hold it untouched for five years: 1M XRP remains 1M XRP.

If the same position hypothetically generated and retained 4% in XRP annually, it would grow to roughly 1,216,653 XRP after five years.

That is around 216,653 additional XRP without the example depending on selling the original position.

Now scale that idea to a treasury starting around 473M XRP.

The SEC filing lays out four ways Evernorth intends to pursue returns.

Institutional XRP lending.

Qualified firms borrow XRP, pay interest, and Evernorth can potentially retain those earnings.

Covered calls.

Evernorth can earn option premiums against portions of its XRP holdings and potentially recycle the net proceeds back into the treasury.

Basis trading.

Professional traders can seek returns from differences between XRP spot, futures and derivatives pricing.

Onchain lending and AMM liquidity.

XRP can eventually be supplied into lending markets or liquidity pools, earning interest, trading fees or eligible incentives.

That last part is the one I think could become huge over time.

Evernorth is preparing XRP to behave more like productive financial capital.

Banks, brokers, hedge funds, liquidity providers and asset managers already understand lending, collateral, financing, derivatives and treasury management.

Evernorth is taking those familiar financial activities and applying them around XRP.

And they even plan to measure it.

The SEC filing identifies Yield Per Token:

XRP generated by a strategy divided by XRP deployed into that strategy.

It also points to XRP generation per share and XRP reserves per share.

So eventually an XRPN investor may ask:

How much XRP was deployed?

How much additional XRP did it generate?

Did net XRP holdings rise?

Did XRP per share increase?

Those are real operating metrics, not just “did XRP price go up?”

Birla has also talked about institutional blockchain adoption needing three things:

Technology. Commercialization. Liquidity.
Ripple and developers build the rails.
Institutional firms bring access and distribution.
Evernorth brings a 473M XRP capital base that can potentially supply liquidity.
Then firms such as Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo provide execution, custody, financing, collateral and market infrastructure around it.

Hugo Jeandesboz brings the kind of derivatives, cross-currency and institutional portfolio experience that fits this model perfectly.

Then XRPL itself is preparing native credit infrastructure.

XLS-65 — Single Asset Vaults

XRP and other supported assets can be pooled into onchain vaults.

XLS-66 — Lending Protocol

Those pools can support fixed-term credit, with borrower assessment handled offchain and loan administration recorded on XRPL.

The October 16 snapshot showed:

SingleAssetVault: 21/35 trusted validators

LendingProtocol: 19/35

29 are required for the relevant majority.

Evernorth has already announced a relationship with Doppler Finance to explore institutional XRP liquidity deployment and onchain treasury infrastructure.

Here is an easy hypothetical.

Evernorth puts 10M XRP into an institutional lending vault.

Borrowers use the capital.

A hypothetical 3% net return produces 300,000 XRP.

Evernorth retains it.

Now the treasury owns more XRP than before.

Repeat similar activity across larger allocations and longer periods and the compounding starts becoming meaningful.

At a hypothetical 4% annual net XRP growth rate, 473M XRP becomes roughly 575.5M XRP after five years.

That is approximately 102.5M additional XRP.

And the metric I would watch closest is XRP per share.

Growing the treasury matters.

Growing XRP attributable to each share matters even more.

Now look at the financial world forming around this strategy.

Brevan Howard, managing roughly $35B, expanded its relationship with Ripple Prime around clearing, prime brokerage and financing.

Standard Chartered launched custody for RLUSD on XRPL and is working with Ripple and LMAX around collateral.

DriveWealth, which serves more than 100 global brokerage partners, is working with Ripple around settlement, cross-border funding, custody and financing.

Virtu is already participating in Ripple Prime’s institutional real-world-asset derivatives offering across more than 100 reference markets.

Then come the tokenized assets that need liquidity around them.

Ondo Finance — OUSG tokenized Treasuries.

Guggenheim Treasury Services / Zeconomy — digital commercial paper.

Aviva Investors — work around tokenized traditional funds.

CSD BR and BTG Pactual — securities-record infrastructure.

Ripple and Securitize — RLUSD access around BlackRock BUIDL and VanEck VBILL.

Tokenized assets need buyers, sellers, funding, settlement and liquidity.

Evernorth wants to be one of the capital providers sitting around that activity.

And the market size is massive.

DTCC: more than $114T in assets custodied by DTC.

McKinsey: around $2T tokenized financial assets by 2030 in its base case, around $4T in an optimistic scenario.

If a $4T tokenized market eventually existed and XRPL serviced 5%, that would represent $200B of assets.

A hypothetical 2% XRP working-capital requirement around that activity would equal $4B in XRP liquidity.

At 10% XRPL participation and 3% working capital, the illustration reaches $12B.

Traditional investors also do not need to become DeFi experts overnight.

XRPN gives them a familiar public-market structure.

Evernorth manages its own XRP treasury, financial strategies and counterparties.

Capital can also enter through structures institutions already understand.

In September, NH Investment & Securities, acting as trustee for Kyobo AIM Corporate Finance General Private Investment Trust No. 3, participated in a $30M convertible senior note carrying 4% payment-in-kind interest and maturing in 2031.

Existing Evernorth backers include SBI Group, Ripple, Arrington Capital, Pantera Capital, Kraken and GSR.

Q4 2026: institutional XRP yield strategies begin.

2027: XRPL lending infrastructure can open another route for productive XRP.

2027–2028: lenders, trading firms, brokers and treasury companies join.

Beyond 2030: XRPL competes for financial activity that historically lived across separate banking, brokerage, credit and settlement systems.

So when people ask me when I would “sell all my XRP,” I think they may be missing another possible future.

What if XRP becomes an asset institutions want to hold, lend, finance against, provide liquidity with and generate income from?

The strongest future would combine:

Persistent institutional XRP accumulation.

Productive XRP holdings.

Independent demand from lenders, market makers, trading firms and treasury managers.

Evernorth now has hundreds of millions of XRP, institutional partners and a strategy built around making that treasury productive.

The next proof comes from the numbers: more XRP generated, more XRP per share and deeper institutional liquidity.

40多万的利润，一下子全部吐干净。最后，不仅利润归零，我最初的10万本金，也一分不剩。

美银的分析师抛了个挺有意思的观点，把英伟达、博通、美光这三家，跟互联网泡沫破裂后的思科放在一起比。

结论是，这三家现在的估值水平，跟思科崩盘后那几年差不多。市场正在用一个“已经崩过的价格”，去定价一个“还没崩的周期”。

这个类比有意思的地方，是思科和英伟达后来走的路完全不一样。思科泡沫破了之后，股价花了很久很久才回到前高，因为它的利润在泡沫后长期停滞。但美银的判断是，英伟达这几家的利润不会重蹈思科的覆辙，还会继续增长。

所以这份报告本质上是在说一件事：市场情绪已经把AI硬件打到了崩盘后的价格，但基本面信号跟当年完全相反。

旧款芯片的租赁价格在涨，利用率接近饱和，大模型平台的调用量还在往上翻。需求端没有冷却，订单也没断，但股价先跌为敬。

我的理解是，这个类比真正的价值，不在像不像思科，在于它逼着你想一个问题：如果估值已经跌到崩盘后的水平，但订单还在往上走，那接下来是估值修复，还是基本面真的会跟着估值一起往下掉？

美银给的答案偏前者。它觉得AI硬件的需求还在加速，而市场情绪反应过度了。

但风险也摆在那里。新云端的信用事件、利率环境、电力和许可的延迟，任何一个都可能变成真正的断点。

我的看法是，这个裂口迟早要收敛，但往哪边收敛，取决于两件事：AI应用的收入能不能跟上，以及融资成本还会不会继续往上走。目前的数据，还停在贵但没事的阶段，但裂口本身，值得盯住。

美银的分析师抛了个挺有意思的观点，把英伟达、博通、美光这三家，跟互联网泡沫破裂后的思科放在一起比。

结论是，这三家现在的估值水平，跟思科崩盘后那几年差不多。市场正在用一个“已经崩过的价格”，去定价一个“还没崩的周期”。

这个类比有意思的地方，是思科和英伟达后来走的路完全不一样。思科泡沫破了之后，股价花了很久很久才回到前高，因为它的利润在泡沫后长期停滞。但美银的判断是，英伟达这几家的利润不会重蹈思科的覆辙，还会继续增长。

所以这份报告本质上是在说一件事：市场情绪已经把AI硬件打到了崩盘后的价格，但基本面信号跟当年完全相反。

旧款芯片的租赁价格在涨，利用率接近饱和，大模型平台的调用量还在往上翻。需求端没有冷却，订单也没断，但股价先跌为敬。

我的理解是，这个类比真正的价值，不在像不像思科，在于它逼着你想一个问题：如果估值已经跌到崩盘后的水平，但订单还在往上走，那接下来是估值修复，还是基本面真的会跟着估值一起往下掉？

美银给的答案偏前者。它觉得AI硬件的需求还在加速，而市场情绪反应过度了。

但风险也摆在那里。新云端的信用事件、利率环境、电力和许可的延迟，任何一个都可能变成真正的断点。

我的看法是，这个裂口迟早要收敛，但往哪边收敛，取决于两件事：AI应用的收入能不能跟上，以及融资成本还会不会继续往上走。目前的数据，还停在贵但没事的阶段，但裂口本身，值得盯住。

美股如今的“财报交易”是一种拥挤的、内卷的，对散户难以形成正 EV 的交易类型。

用期权买方向，财报日的高 IV 瞬间坍塌会消耗大量的利润；用期货买方向，财报日上下乱插针，长期容易沦为主力的流动性。

财报，本质上无法改变一个股票的趋势。

上涨趋势中，好财报 = 继续涨，坏财报 = 回调后继续涨。

下跌趋势中，好财报 = 反弹后继续跌，坏财报 = 继续跌。

以 $SNDK 为例，前一次处于上涨趋势中，后一次处于下跌趋势中。

以个人的交易经验，反而认为“逆势”的财报是更好的交易机会。上涨趋势中的坏财报会产生好的加仓机会；下跌趋势中的好财报会产生好的做空机会。

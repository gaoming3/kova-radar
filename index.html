Blackstone's new data center trust is buying a 75% stake in two $AMZN data center campuses valued at about $5 billion. The deal covers 13 newly built data center buildings with 647 megawatts of power capacity, nine in northern Indiana near Chicago and four in Mississippi.

Amazon keeps the other 25% and will lease back all 13 buildings for at least 12 years, with options that can stretch to 32. The price works out to a 7% initial yield, and the rent rises 2.75% a year.

Meta did a version of this last year, when funds run by Blue Owl took an 80% stake in its $27 billion Hyperion data center campus in Louisiana.

It is the first deal for Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust, which raised $1.75 billion in its IPO in May. It is expected to close by year-end.

Hey friends

I came across something interesting while exploring @Canborsa_DEX , and I thought it was worth sharing with you all.

Imagine trading gold, BTC, or even stocks without selling your $CC first.

Here's how it works:

Instead of converting your CC into stablecoins before opening a trade, you can use your existing CC as margin.

• Deposit your CC as collateral while keeping your exposure to it.
• Your CC is valued at its current market sell price, without an additional discount. This determines your available margin.
• Your position size depends on your margin and chosen leverage.

Position size = Margin × Leverage.

This works across supported markets, from crypto and gold to stocks like Nvidia.

Now, what happens when you close your position?

If you're in profit, your earnings are paid in stablecoins, and your original CC is returned.

If the trade goes against you, only the amount of CC needed to cover the loss is sold. Any remaining CC is returned.

But there's one important detail to understand.

You're now dealing with two different price movements: your actual trade and the market price of CC.

If CC drops in value, your liquidation price can move closer even if your trading position hasn't changed.

That's why managing risk matters. Start with a small position, keep leverage low, and always consider setting a stop-loss.

It's an interesting way to use your $CC as trading collateral while maintaining exposure to it, without having to sell it upfront.

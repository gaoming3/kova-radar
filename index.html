This never ends WELL.

5 of history's biggest stock market bubbles all ended in a crash or a recession.

Today, the 41 stocks JPMorgan classifies as AI-related make up 49% of the S&P 500.

That's higher than every one of those bubbles except the 1880s railroads.

Here's how each of them ended:

- Railroads, 63% in 1881: the US fell into the Depression of 1882 to 1885

- Roaring Twenties, 36% in 1929: the Dow fell 89% by 1932 and took 25 years to recover

- Nifty Fifty, 40% in 1972: the S&P 500 fell 48% in 1973 and 1974

- Japan, 44% in 1988: the Nikkei peaked a year later, lost over 80% and took 34 years to recover

- Dot-com, 41% in 2000: the Nasdaq fell 78% and took 15 years to recover

That's 15, 25 and 34 years for those indexes to reclaim their peaks.

Meanwhile, Bianco Research says AI would already be 51% of the S&P 500 if SpaceX were included.

"The last time a single theme was >50% of the $SPX was the railroads in the 19th century," the firm adds.

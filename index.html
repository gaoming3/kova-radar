You could buy 100 shares of $AVGO right now for about $33,957.

Or you could buy the $300 call expiring January 2028 for roughly $94, which is $9,410 for one contract.

That's leveraged exposure to 100 shares of Broadcom for about 72% less capital, with 493 days of runway.

Strike: $300
Expiration: January 21, 2028
Premium: about $94 per share
Breakeven: $394, about 16% above where the stock trades today

$39.57 of that premium is already intrinsic value. You're paying about $54 in time value for 16 months.

If $AVGO hits $450 by expiration, the LEAP returns roughly 59% versus 33% on shares.
If $AVGO gets back to its 52 week high of $495, the LEAP returns roughly 107% versus 46% on shares.
If $AVGO hits $550, the LEAP returns roughly 166% versus 62% on shares.

Why I like the setup:

- AI semiconductor revenue was $16.7B last quarter, up 221%, with this quarter guided to $21.7B
- Hock Tan said Broadcom has already secured the supply to double AI revenue to roughly $115B in 2027, with line of sight to $230B in 2028
- Anthropic is set to become the largest custom chip customer in 2027 with an incremental 10 gigawatts in 2028, and OpenAI is deploying over 5 gigawatts in 2028
- Free cash flow was $13.7B last quarter, 46% of revenue
- The stock is 31% below its June high with the AI selloff, so you are buying the same growth at a lower entry
- 493 days of runway covers five more earnings reports

The max you can lose on a LEAP is the entire premium you paid. In this case that's $9,410 per contract. Broadcom has already dropped 31% from its high in three months, and if it stays flat or keeps sliding this contract loses value every day. Only size this so you're comfortable losing all of it.

Note: LEAPs are one tool inside a broader portfolio. Owning shares is always the primary use of capital. This is a selective add-on for high conviction moments when conditions align.

$ETH 现在还没有突破 4 年前的高点，一整个周期里都被大家嫌弃。

但 Arthur Hayes 在最近的播客节目中公开表示，现在 ETH 是自己的第一大仓位，也透露了自己重仓的逻辑，和对当前宏观经济的判断：

1.逆向交易，ETH 被嫌弃就是溢价

在 Arthur 看来，“被嫌弃”本身就是一种买入溢价。 他的逻辑很现实：如果你想在比特币之外追求高弹性，但又不想承担那些新协议“一夜跌去 75%”的暴雷风险，大市值里只有 ETH 可选。

涨上天的标的利好早已兑现，而趴在谷底的资产反而“没有预期要还”。除了 ETH，他还配置了小仓位的 ETHFI 和 ENA。

2.BTC 要走到 25 万美元，政客们需要一个借口

为什么 BTC 前段时间只能从 6.3 万走到 8 万？因为当前美联储的资产负债表扩张，相比 2008 年或 2020 年根本算不上“疯狂”。 Arthur 指出，美联储想要开启真正的量化宽松（QE），政客们必须给民众一个交代。这意味着，市场必须先经历一场“真危机”来祭旗，才能换来后续史诗级的流动性开闸。

3.盯紧“欧元兑日元”汇率

这场危机可能从哪里引爆？Arthur 给出了一个非常具体的观测指标：欧元兑日元（EUR/JPY）汇率。 这里的传导链条是：

日本人在抛售资产，美联储必须借出美元来稳住局面。

同时，法国三大银行占据了美国回购市场 20% 的份额，且日本人手里捏着大量法国国债。

一旦欧元兑日元从 182 跌至 140 甚至 120，法国的银行体系将率先撑不住。在欧盟框架内无法合法印钱的法国，最终大概率只能选择自己搞量化宽松（软性退出欧元区）。

所以，他设想的终极剧本是，法日债务链条的断裂将引发全球连环爆雷，从而倒逼央行们以“拯救经济”之名开启印钞机... 最终推动 BTC 价格上涨。

不过这感觉有点远，更像是幻想，Arthur 这波喊单能先让 ETH 硬起来吗🤔？

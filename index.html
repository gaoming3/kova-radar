$MU earnings in about 24 hours, and many of you know Micron is notorious for dipping after earnings.

But I'm not sure the bar is high anymore. There's been so much China and Apple FUD (still is), and so much "top" narrative saying Micron should miss the guide and roll into a downturn. None of the published projection data supports that.

Most people still don't grasp the exponential curve of Agentic AI. At the very least, it can hold DRAM prices where they are, as long as it keeps delivering real value through automation. And let's be honest, prices can't go much higher from here anyway. Push them too far and AI buildout projects get cancelled, which is bearish for memory. So "hold here" is the healthy scenario.

Then there's LTAs. I honestly think the market doesn't know how to price them. The price isn't fixed, but there's a floor, and it sits above any previous cycle high. The only way to prove it is for Micron to keep printing 80%+ margins for the next few years. Then the market can finally rerate it.

Hyperscalers are locked into five year LTAs with deposits and price floors. TrendForce reports Samsung has ~70% of capacity tied up through 2031. The memory trio grows supply ~20% a year, and most new 2027 supply is already sold. When less than half the supply ever reaches the open market, what's left has very little room to fall.

2028 projections? Let's revisit once we're actually in 2027 and new data comes out. That's too far out to call.

My take: we're about to shift from price driven growth to sustained shipment growth in 2027+. That's what a flattened cycle looks like.

All in all, Burry converted his shorts to puts. More leverage.

Let's see who's smiling on the other side.

$ASTS: JAPAN'S RAKUTEN TO DEBUT SATELLITE-TO-CELL SERVICE WITH STARLINK RIVAL AST - NIKKEI

Space-based network rolls out as soon as this year, will support video calls

TAKAHIRO YAMAMOTO
September 5, 2026 01:20 JST

TOKYO -- Japan's Rakuten Group plans to launch a direct-to-mobile satellite communications service as early as this year through a partnership with U.S. company AST SpaceMobile, competing directly with SpaceX's Starlink system.

The tech group and AST SpaceMobile will establish a company in Japan this year that operates the satellites. Satellite telecommunications offer various applications including disaster recovery. The partners also envision expanding the business beyond Japan.

AST SpaceMobile, founded in Texas in 2017, went public on Nasdaq in 2021. Abel Avellan, the chairman, CEO and founder, previously managed another satellite communications firm.

When Rakuten made a full entry into the mobile business in 2020, the Japanese group formed a strategic partnership with the U.S. company that included an investment.

AST SpaceMobile's satellite communications technology can support video streaming as well as voice calls. In a trial conducted with Rakuten last year, the partners enabled video calls via satellite.

The antennas attached to the satellites are large, spanning roughly 223 square meters. The size enables high-speed data communications even with a small number of satellites. It is estimated that 90 units would be sufficient to provide services worldwide.

The U.S. company plans to put 45 satellites into orbit by early 2027. The company launched three in August, extending the constellation to 13 units.

Elon Musk's SpaceX, a pioneer in this field, has launched over 10,000 satellites for its Starlink service. But Starlink's network does not yet support video calls. AST SpaceMobile will have a strong selling point if it becomes the first to feasibly offer video calls.

Rakuten also touts the national security benefits of the collaboration with the U.S. partner. The new joint venture will have a nearly 50-50 split, and Rakuten will take the lead in the project.

"It's extremely critical to launch a service with a company that has the significant backing of Japanese capital," said Hiroshi Mikitani, Rakuten's chairman and CEO.

Japan's government is supporting Rakuten's push into homegrown satellite telecommunications. In June, the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications said it selected Rakuten's consortium to receive up to 150 billion yen ($962 million) in subsidies.

As a late entrant in Japan's mobile sector, Rakuten has struggled to develop a network of base stations, especially in rural areas. Rakuten has a roaming agreement with KDDI, but the coverage shrinks in October due to the expiration of the deal.

Satellite communication services would let Rakuten supplement parts of the existing mobile network. The challenge involves how far Rakuten can catch up with Starlink.

Last year, KDDI launched a direct-to-mobile satellite communications service using Starlink. Domestic rivals SoftBank Corp. and NTT Docomo started similar services in 2026.

KDDI's user base for mobile satellite communications topped 4 million people as of April, with the service expanding to corporate clients. NTT Docomo had 6.5 million subscribers as of the end of July.

我先说一个总体看法那就是首先需要关注的就是截图里提到的GB300 NVL72到Rubin Ultra NVL576，即使假设CPO渗透率为29%，那每计算单元的可插拔光模块数量将从GB300 NVL72的216个增加到Rubin Ultra NVL576的2,500个。

这里我们需要去结合一下nvda那边的说法Rubin Ultra 的芯片大概率 2027 年出, 但完整的 Kyber NVL576 整柜有滑向 2028 的风险，而其中的问题是受限于封装和高层 HBM 良率。

另外Jensen 对于2027 年的 Rubin NVL576 机柜的态度是“We’re going to do both”，所以他们纵向仍用铜、横向骨干用的是”可插拔”光模块, 而不是 CPO。

所以这意味着nvda的态度是scale-up能用铜就用铜这里指就是刚刚说的NVLink、CPO 先落在交换机/scale-out 层、可插拔光模块依然是十年主力、光进 GPU 要等 Feynman/2028。

那么结合我刚刚说的我们去看高盛图里的细节，他们是把这套逻辑量化到了每一代:

scale-up 一路到 Vera Rubin NVL72 仍是铜这一个和我之前的nvda文章说的一样, 然后只有到密度爆炸的 Rubin Ultra (NVL144→576) 才被逼向 PCB midplane、再到”铜+CPO”;

scale-out 从纯可插拔1.6T过渡到”CPO TOR 交换机 + 光模块”;

而且即便到 Rubin Ultra, CPO 渗透率也只给到 25–29%，这一点和我nvda文章里 LightCounting”2030 年才约 35%“的口径完全一个方向。

那你从这里可以看到CPO是分层放量、可插拔会是持续十年的主力，而这一份高盛研报是这一逻辑的支持者: 密度从 72 卡爆到 576 卡, 你能发现整个连接的盘子涨太快, 所以就算 CPO 咬走 scale-out 的三成, 可插拔的绝对用量还是翻了 10 倍。

你可以从表里”optical module”这一行也印证: 从 GB300 的 8.3 b涨到 Rubin Ultra 的 32.4 b, 在CPO 起量的同时自己还在长。

所以报告在反复告诉你CPO 不是替代可插拔, 而是总蛋糕做大后两者一起涨，这也是我 6 月那波光模块错杀里想说的话。

高盛标了 15 b TAM”主要在 2026”(GB300)、154 b”主要在 2028”(Rubin Ultra NVL576), 9 倍增长踩在 2026 → 2028 这条线上。这跟我文里光学伙伴的确认节奏也就是 Coherent scale-out CPO 从 2H26、scale-up 到 2027;Lumentum scale-up 2027、部署 2028)是同一条斜率

所以钱不是消失了，而是从 2026 一路铺到 2028 按层确认”, 然后高盛这张表就是那条斜率的最好版本。

另外俩个需要看的点：

第一个是激光/光引擎/FAU 这条”卡点”链

表里 ELS从 $0→$10 b、optical engine & FAU 从 $0→$56b。这也是我文章里和 FN 财报说的”激光/关键零部件是当前真正卡点”的证据, 而这一块对应的是 SIVE / COHR / LITE / FN

scale-out 从纯可插拔1.6T过渡到”CPO TOR 交换机 + 光模块”;

那你从这里可以看到CPO是分层放量、可插拔会是持续十年的主力，而这一份高盛研报是这一逻辑的支持者: 密度从 72 卡爆到 576 卡, 你能发现整个连接的盘子涨太快, 所以就算 CPO 咬走 scale-out 的三成, 可插拔的绝对用量还是翻了 10 倍。

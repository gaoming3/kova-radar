4. always buy 1-strike OTM.
never ATM. never deep OTM.

5. set your stop loss before you enter.
not after. not during. before.
bracket order. +20% TP. -12.5% SL.

6. learn what RSI is telling you.
RSI > 70 at resistance = puts.

not the market — you.

8. learn how SPY moves impact your option price.
this is the most important thing nobody teaches.

a $0.50 SPY move at a fresh level
can move your option +15 to +25%.
a $0.20 SPY move might do nothing.

1-strike OTM 0DTE has a delta of roughly 0.25 to 0.35.
that means every $1 SPY moves — your option moves $0.25 to $0.35.

so before every trade ask:
how far does SPY need to move for me to hit my target?

$1.00 option. +20% target = exit at $1.20.
that's a $0.20 move on the option.
at delta 0.30 — SPY needs to move $0.55 to $0.70 in your favor.

at a fresh level with RSI confirming — that's realistic in 1 to 5 minutes.
at a weak level with no confirmation — that's a lottery ticket.

9. size small until you're consistent.
$200-$300 campaigns max until you have 20 clean sessions.
protect the account first.
grow it second.

10. document everything.
every trade. every win. every loss. every rule you broke.
the pattern in your losses will tell you exactly what to fix.

the market will always be there tomorrow.
your account won't if you're not careful.

good luck out there.
wishing all you 0DTE traders nothing but success

My thoughts on the current state of crypto/markets (and what's next):

• In general, the world is in a volatile/scary place right now. Cyberattacks, FAA flight chaos, etc. I think people are really starting to worry about where they can store capital (this thesis is backed by the general stock market dip)
• In hindsight, the Binance hack was a huge bear signal for crypto. Stablecoin supply fell 5%+ after the hack. Whenever this happens, it's typically a huge bear signal for crypto and liquidity flowing out. This trust takes a while to rebuild and there is usually a delayed reaction which we are seeing now.
• Crypto ran on the debasement narrative. Now the narrative has shifted to the AI buildout struggling/growth fears, and that's weighing on every macro asset.
• The breakout to the 80s was a classic bull trap (deviation above range high). I think a lot of people got trapped. For me, losing the $74K range low was the real bear signal in terms of market structure. There is major support at $60k. Expecting this to hold, but if it doesn't things would get very ugly (not my base case for now).
• My $BTC plan: wait for an uptrend to form on the 4H and look for a swing into the high 70s. If not, I'll bid on the weekly timeframe close to $60K with inval below. Breaking $60K would be like breaking $40K in 2022: pure capitulation.
• For a real move back to the upside, we'd need a meaningful pivot in monetary policy. That can happen, but right now the Fed is hawkish (and only getting more so).
• All eyes on macro/equities rn. If stocks are weak, BTC/crypto is going to struggle (most of the relative strength is gone now - a big reason being the Binance hack)
• In terms of alts, I'm only really interested in laddering into $BTC, $HYPE & $ZEC. I also think these take time but from a long term POV I think they are solid assets to DCA and hold. There are 5-6 other assets I'm interested in, but until the overall market stabilizes they're relatively uninvestable imo (unless you're willing to cop big downside). Overall, I'd say we're closer to the bottom now, but that doesn't necessarily R/R favours heavily deploying here. As I said before I'd rather wait for some strength than catch a knife (with the exception of some light long term DCAs).
• From a business POV, I'm 90% focused on AI now. Imo, building a business is always the best hedge against volatile markets. There is so much opportunity there, which is exciting. And automated trading of course. There's going to be a massive shift in financial markets with agents, and it's a very fun intersection to build in.
• Advice: don't trade shaky markets. Just focus on upskilling and increasing active income to trade in better market conditions. But also don't be afraid to take advantage of asymmetric opportunities if they present.

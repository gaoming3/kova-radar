$ASTS: 🚨 AST SPACEMOBILE ANNOUNCES SUCCESSFUL ORBITAL LAUNCH OF BLUEBIRDS 11, 12, AND 13

Next-generation satellites expand AST SpaceMobile’s space-based cellular broadband network for commercial and government applications

BlueBird satellites are the largest communications arrays ever deployed in low Earth orbit, expected to support peak data rates approaching 200 Mbps directly to everyday smartphones

BlueBirds 14, 15, and 16 are preparing for the next mission, with production advancing through BlueBird satellite 42

MIDLAND, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--AST SpaceMobile, Inc. ("AST SpaceMobile") (NASDAQ: ASTS), the company building the first and only space-based cellular broadband network accessible directly by everyday smartphones, designed for both commercial and government applications, today announced the successful orbital launch of its BlueBird satellites 11, 12, and 13.

The mission lifted off at 3:42 a.m. EDT on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station aboard a Falcon 9 rocket, expanding AST SpaceMobile’s growing constellation of next-generation BlueBird satellites following the successful launch and deployment of BlueBirds 8, 9, and 10. With BlueBirds 14, 15, and 16 preparing for the next mission and production advancing through BlueBird satellite 42, the company continues to execute its constellation deployment program at scale.

BlueBird satellites 11, 12, and 13 are the largest communications arrays ever deployed in low Earth orbit, more than three times the size of the company’s five initial Block 1 BlueBird satellites. The large apertures and advanced beamforming capabilities enable greater targeted coverage, reduced interference, and increased capacity, supporting reliable cellular broadband connectivity directly to standard, unmodified smartphones. These next-generation satellites are expected to deliver peak data rates approaching 200 Mbps, nearly double the 98.9 Mbps achieved by the company's five initial Block 1 BlueBird satellites, supporting voice, broadband data, and video applications from space.

“The successful launch of BlueBirds 11, 12, and 13 demonstrates the progress of a space-based cellular broadband network built for scale,” said Abel Avellan, Founder, Chairman, and CEO of AST SpaceMobile. “We are repeatedly building and deploying the largest phased arrays in low Earth orbit. Our unique technology is designed to provide broadband for direct-to-device but is increasingly serving as a platform for additional capabilities. We remain focused on scaling our cellular broadband network as we prepare for beta services later this year.”

Each launch advances the company’s goal of delivering seamless, space-based cellular broadband integrated with terrestrial mobile networks, leveraging a flexible spectrum strategy across both partner and AST SpaceMobile spectrum, together with standard 4G and 5G devices.

As the constellation grows, the company progresses toward initial service activation and continuous coverage across key markets including the United States, Canada, Europe, Saudi Arabia, and Japan, as well as the U.S. Government and other strategic markets.

AST SpaceMobile has agreements with nearly 60 mobile network operators globally representing more than 3 billion subscribers combined, together with strategic partnerships with AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, Rakuten, Google, Bell, Telus, stc Group, and American Tower.

BlueBird satellites are assembled, integrated, and tested at AST SpaceMobile’s facilities in Midland Texas, reflecting the company’s commitment to U.S.-based innovation and manufacturing. AST SpaceMobile now operates over 500,000 square feet of manufacturing and operations facilities worldwide, supported by a 2,250+ workforce, and a technology platform backed by over 3,900 patents and patent-pending claims.

About AST SpaceMobile

AST SpaceMobile is building the first and only global cellular broadband network in space to operate directly with standard, unmodified mobile devices based on our extensive IP and patent portfolio, and designed for both commercial and government applications. Our engineers and space scientists are on a mission to enable 4G and 5G space-based cellular broadband to every device, everywhere, for today’s nearly 6 billion mobile subscribers globally. Google 最不能走的人,走了。 $GOOGL

WSJ:Google 首席科学家 Jeff Dean 离职创办自己的 AI 公司,据快讯另有三名资深 AI 老将同行。同一批消息:Hassabis 卸任 DeepMind CEO,CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu 接棒。$GOOG 应声大跌。

先说 Jeff Dean 是谁——他不是一个高管,他是 Google 的地基本身。

1999 年入职,第 30 号员工,公司第一批博士。MapReduce、BigTable、Spanner、TensorFlow、TPU,全是他主导或共同设计的。Google 内部有个流传多年的梗系列叫"Jeff Dean Facts",最出名的一条:"真空光速曾经只有 35 英里每小时,直到 Jeff Dean 花了一个周末优化物理。"

27 年。搜索时代他建了索引的地基,AI 时代他建了算力的地基。这样的人今天离职创业,市场跌得不冤。

但三个细节比恐慌更值得看:

一、这不是冲动,是筹备已久。 Fortune 之前报道过,Dean 过去两年已经悄悄天使投资了 37 家 AI 初创——Perplexity、World Labs、Sakana AI 都有他的钱。地图早就画好了,今天只是本人下场。

二、真正的信号是数学,不是人事。 Ilya 出走做 SSI,估值 $320 亿;Mira 的 Thinking Machines 融资几十亿;Leopold 管到 $240 亿(虽然上周刚爆仓)。一个 AI 传奇的名字,本身就值一轮独角兽估值——任何薪酬包都跑不赢这个数学。巨头的天才从来是租的,不是买断的,只是 AI 资本市场第一次把租约价格标这么高。

三、别把人才地震直接换算成研究力崩塌。 反例就在 Google 自己身上:Transformer 论文八位作者,几年前就全部离开了 Google,Gemini 照样做了出来。组织的复利有时候大于个人。Hassabis 那条若属实,更可能是退向科学线(他的心一直在 AlphaFold 和 Isomorphic 那边),Koray 是 2012 年就加入 DeepMind 的内部连续性人选——这部分等官方细节,先别按"管理层崩了"定价。

一个参照点:两个月前 Berkshire 私募进 GOOGL 的成本是 Class A $351.81。今天这根阴线,正在测试 Buffett 团队算了九个月的那笔账。

巨头留不住传奇,是这个时代的定价,不是 Google 的独家问题。真正该盯的是:他的新公司做什么方向、带走的是哪三个人。名单比眼泪值钱。

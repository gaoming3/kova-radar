The amazing thing is that despite everyone bitching about Gulf Coast 3-2-1 prompt cracks in high $50s, refiners aren’t actually earning good returns currently. Add back $15 of RIN cost and they’re earning $40 cracks. Pull out $10 of refining + logistics cost and you have $30 margins. Subtract $3/bbl of maintenance cost and you’re at $27. Now use 90% yield and you’re at $24. Capacity costs ~$30,000 per bbl to build today (and takes 5-7 years). You get ~90% average up-time. So $24 margin X 330 days = $7,920 of margin per unit of refining capacity. It’s a 26.4% return on the $30,000 build cost before you have to finance your inventory, cover corporate SG&A and whatnot (just look at the idiotic special tax that $MPC agreed to yesterday in California).

Call it a low/mid 20% annualized return for the highest cracks in a lifetime. These refiners aren’t over-earning by any stretch. Go look at the $MPC or $VLO indicator sheets on their websites. And these are the best run refiners in the world. Their returns on replacement cost so far this year are mid teens.

Why is no one adding capacity?? Because until 6 months ago, returns on capital were single digits. The share prices are still well below replacement cost. Why add capacity when you can do buybacks at a lower cost per bbl of capacity?? Wake me when these things trade at 1.5x or 2x capacity ($45k -$60k per bbl of capacity) and then maybe supply comes. That’s still a long way away.

So much of the industrial economy has under-earned for over a decades. There hasn’t been supply added because the market penalized supply. Then an event happens and the market realizes that supply was actually tight.

You need more than 2 months at low/mid 20s annualized returns on capital before supply has a response. Some call this inflation. Others call this gross margin. Others call it a return to acceptable returns on capital. You need retained earnings for many quarters, followed by visibility on future cracks before supply has a response. When you can hedge 2035-2045 cracks at $60, then the supply comes. Until then, who the hell would finance a supply response??

For investors, go figure out who’s been under-earning for years. For policymakers, extending the Small Refiner Exemption to all refiners, and it will immediately reduce prices for consumers. A lot of the problems with diesel, are caused by idiotic policy decisions forcing guys to run biodiesel plants at a loss, subsidized by a handful of large refiners. For everyone else, just remember that a functioning economy means that everyone has to eventually earn their cost of capital…

这也是我最近重新看 Stock 的一个原因。

不是说股票就一定比 Crypto 好，而是 Crypto 玩久了之后，很多人开始发现：自己真正想交易的，可能不是“一个币”，而是一个明确的事件。

比如今晚财报。

比如某个行业突然出利好。

比如 AI、芯片、汽车这些板块突然集体波动。

这种情况下，我觉得 @MEXC 的 Stock Futures 反而是个挺直接的工具。

它不是让你去“持有一家公司的股票”。

你交易的是价格波动。

看涨可以做多，看跌也可以做空，用 USDT 结算。截至 2026 年 8 月，MEXC 已经有 300+ 个 Stock 相关标的，包括个股、ETF 和指数。

我觉得这里最重要的区别，其实很多人一直没搞明白。

买股票的人，关心的是这家公司以后怎么样。

做 Stock Futures 的人，关心的是接下来这段行情怎么走。

完全是两套逻辑。

比如财报前，你不一定要真的长期看好这家公司，也没必要买完以后放几年。

你只是觉得市场预期可能太高，财报出来后存在下跌压力。

那你研究的就是一段行情，而不是“我要不要成为这家公司的股东”。

这也是我觉得 Stock Futures 比较适合 Crypto 老玩家的地方。

因为我们本来就习惯了交易波动。

当然，别看到“最高 200x 杠杆”就开始上头。

杠杆不是送钱工具，方向错了照样亏，甚至可能被强平，另外还有资金费用这些成本需要考虑。

真正有用的不是把杠杆开多大，而是你能不能分清楚：自己到底是在投资，还是在交易。

另外，MEXC 的 Stock 相关产品有 Zero Fee / 0 Fee 优势，但具体适用产品和活动条件还是要看官方最新页面。Zero Fee 不等于没有任何交易风险，也不代表所有成本都不存在。

我自己的看法很简单：

如果只是为了找下一个能翻几十倍的小币，那 Stock Futures 可能没那么刺激。

但如果你开始厌倦每天追各种消息盘、流动性差的小币，却又还是喜欢做行情、做事件、做多空判断，那 Stock Futures 可能值得放进你的工具箱。

从盯一堆不知道什么时候归零的小币，到开始研究真实市场里的财报、行业和事件。

对交易员来说，这其实是另一种选择。

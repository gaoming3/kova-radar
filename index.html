Robinhood says they are backed 1:1. That is Robinhood’s claim. There is no public proof-of-reserves style attestation that retail can check. A 1:1 claim only helps listed $AMC if new tokens are created by buying real shares on the tape. If they are minted against inventory, locates, hedges, or delayed settlement, the market gets extra AMC exposure without extra demand hitting NYSE.

The on-chain AMC wrapper is tiny and easy to shove around. Recent figures put the Robinhood AMC token around $298,000 in value, about 113,000 tokens, and roughly 1,260 holders. That is not the whole company. It is a thin second float using the company’s name.

That thin float is already being used as casino plumbing. On Sept. 2, memecoin pairs using Robinhood stock tokens did about $217 million in volume, more than the $127 million in direct stock-token trading. One example: tokenized HIMS got pushed to about $132 while the real stock closed near $29, until more tokens were minted and the gap collapsed. Reports also said a memecoin pool locked around 45% of tokenized AMC. That is how a small wrapper becomes a pressure point.

This is not the same crime as FTX. Nobody should blur that. But retail has seen this movie: a branded token, a real-world name, a thin float, weak public verification, and a parallel market next to the asset people actually own. FTX used FTT as a related-party chip. This uses AMC’s ticker as the chip. Different facts. Same warning. Do not let an unauthorized wrapper become another way to trade AMC without going through AMC’s actual market.

@CEOAdam needs to follow through. Counsel looking into it is the start, not the finish. Ask who can mint and redeem, what is actually in custody, whether that backing is independently verified, and how token dislocations are stopped from leaking into listed AMC.

@SECGov @SECPaulSAtkins @HesterPeirce @FINRA @CFTC this is the issue: an unregistered offshore AMC wrapper, 24/7 trading, DeFi collateral use, and a float small enough to manipulate. Investor protection is not anti-innovation. It is making sure “AMC” means AMC.

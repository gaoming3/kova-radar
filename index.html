I'm probably going to get some hate for saying this, especially from people who follow me for trading.

But over the last few months, God has been answering a prayer of mine in a way I honestly wasn't prepared for.

And it's forced me to confront something about myself that I'm not particularly proud of... “For what does it profit a man to gain the whole world and forfeit his soul?” - Mark 8:36

I've been wrestling with this verse a lot lately, and this weekend I had a pretty uncomfortable realization about myself.

I sat down and started writing out the things I genuinely value most in life. My relationship with the Lord, my family, the people I love, serving others, being present in my community, building meaningful relationships, etc. You know what was near the very bottom of that list? The stock market.

And yet, if someone were to look at how I spend my time, what consumes my thoughts, and what I give my attention to throughout the day, they might come to a completely different conclusion.

That's been a pretty humbling thing to admit.

Over the last 2+ years, I've started realizing just how quickly life moves. Weeks turn into months, months turn into years, and before you know it, you've spent an enormous amount of your life chasing something that was never supposed to be the center of it in the first place. I LOVE trading. The markets have changed my life, opened doors I never imagined, and introduced me to some incredible people.

But I've also realized that something can be a tremendous blessing in your life and still become an idol if you aren't careful.

The Lord has been opening my eyes to this over the last 2.5 months, especially as I've stepped into a new season of life. I've been forced to confront the difference between what I SAY my priorities are and what my daily habits actually reveal. And man, those 2 things haven't always lined up.

I think that's a question worth asking yourself every once in a while... If someone watched how you spent your last 7 days, would they be able to identify what you claim matters most to you?

Because I've had to ask myself that question, and I haven't always liked the answer.

I also think this applies directly to trading. If you come into this industry believing that profitability is eventually going to make you feel whole, you're setting yourself up for a pretty miserable existence. You hit 1 account milestone, feel good for a few days, and immediately start chasing the next one. The goalposts keep moving. I've experienced that firsthand. There's always another trade, another opportunity, another $$ to make, another person doing better than you.

If your fulfillment is entirely dependent on your P&L, you're handing the market control over something it was never designed to give you.

And especially in a difficult market environment, I think some people desperately need to hear that.

It's okay to close the laptop, to miss a trade. It's also okay to spend an afternoon with your family, go serve somebody who can't do anything for you in return, pick up a new hobby, or just sit in silence without needing to accomplish something.

I've been trying to get better at taking my anxious thoughts captive and bringing them before God instead of immediately reaching for my phone, checking another chart, or finding another distraction. It's a daily struggle, but slowly I feel like the veil is being lifted from my eyes.

I'm 22. I have an absurd amount of life left to learn, Lord willing, and I certainly don't have this figured out. But I do know that when I'm standing before Jesus one day, He won't ask me what my equity curve looked like or how much money I made trading $ARM this year.

I want to hear, “Well done, good and faithful servant.”

That's the kind of wealth I'm ultimately after.

So take a little time this weekend to examine your priorities. Not just what you say matters, but what your calendar, attention, thoughts, and actions say matters.

Yes, make money, build businesses, work hard, steward what God has given you. There's nothing inherently wrong with any of those things!! Just don't get so consumed building a life that you forget to actually live it.

The market will be here tomorrow. The people you love won't be here forever.

God bless each of you.

- iain 🩵

炒美股这么久，如果你还只会打开券商软件看涨跌，那真的错过了太多有用的信息！今天整理6个我认为每个美股投资者都值得收藏的网站，从筛选股票、分析财报，到查看机构持仓和美联储利率预期，基本覆盖日常投资研究最重要的几个环节。

很多人每天花几个小时刷财经新闻，却不知道专业投资者真正重要的工作之一，是把分散的信息整理成能够验证的投资判断。

① Finviz
网站：https://t.co/fSNwbyrtuC
美股选股神器！可以根据市值、PE、营收增长、EPS增长、成交量、行业等条件筛选股票。

比如想找市值超过100亿美元、营收增长超过20%、股价接近52周新高的公司，就可以用筛选器快速建立自己的股票观察名单。

② TradingView
网站：https://t.co/3gj7oCnIfL

我认为研究美股技术面非常实用的网站。K线、成交量、均线、RSI、MACD、趋势线都能看，还能设置价格提醒。
研究$MU、$SNDK这种波动较大的股票，可以重点观察成交量变化、关键支撑阻力和突破后的走势。

③ Stock Analysis
网站：https://t.co/aBhQHzhtzm

看公司基本面非常方便！营收、净利润、EPS、自由现金流、资产负债表、估值和分析师预测都能找到。

例如研究$GOOG和$META，可以直接对比两家公司过去几年的收入增长、利润率和自由现金流。
④ SEC EDGAR
网站：https://t.co/Xe05eKmgb3

这是美国证券交易委员会的官方披露系统。上市公司的10-K年报、10-Q季报、8-K重大事项公告，以及高管交易文件都可以查询。

最重要的是，很多媒体报道的消息都能在这里找到原始披露。想确认公司到底有没有宣布回购、增发或者重大交易，建议直接查原文件。

⑤ WhaleWisdom
网站：https://t.co/9f8yPob52Z
想知道巴菲特、知名对冲基金和大型机构持有什么股票？这个网站可以查看13F机构持仓披露。

但要注意，13F最多可以在季度结束45天后才提交，所以看到的并不是机构实时仓位。

⑥ CME FedWatch
网站：https://t.co/RKiQ0dh83y

美股投资者研究利率预期很有用的网站。它根据联邦基金期货价格计算市场隐含的美联储利率调整概率。
每次CPI、非农公布之后，都可以观察市场对下一次FOMC会议的预期是否发生变化。

这6个网站我认为值得反复使用，尤其是Finviz、Stock Analysis和SEC，很多基本研究工作完全可以从这里开始。

炒美股最大的优势，从来不是谁掌握了什么神秘内幕，而是谁能够更快找到可靠的数据，再从中形成自己的判断。

建议收藏，之后研究任何一只美股都能用得上！

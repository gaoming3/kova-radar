Sean trades@SRxTrades21h
Regardless if you like to trade the downside or not, every trader should know how to run a short book when the market is trending below its moving averages.

A lot of traders think shorting means chasing a stock down after 4 or 5 big red candles...

BUT

That’s the easiest way to get caught in a violent snapback rally.

Just like I don't chase extended breakouts on the long side, I have zero interest in blindly shorting stocks that are already stretched 3x ADR below their 8 EMA.

I want the setup to come to ME.

When $SPY and $QQQ are trading below their declining 8, 21, and 50 EMAs, I focus on 3 specific short setups:

1. Failed Breakouts (RDR)

A stock pushes above a key daily resistance level or the previous day's high, sucks in breakout buyers, and immediately fails back into the range.

Everyone who chased the breakout is now trapped underwater, and their stops fuel the move lower.

- Entry: Reversal back below the breakout level / previous day's high
- Stop: High of day (HOD)

2. Bear Flags

Stocks don't go straight down. They move like a staircase:

Large move lower on expanding volume ---> weak bounce or sideways consolidation on light volume ---> breakdown.

I want price staying trapped underneath the declining 8/21 EMAs while volume dries up on the bounce, telling me buyers aren't stepping in aggressively.

- Entry: Break of the lower boundary of the flag as selling volume expands
- Stop: Above the top of the flag consolidation

3. EMA Rejections + 30-Minute Pivot

This is my favorite way to execute a sniper entry on the short side.

Instead of shorting into the hole, wait for a weak stock in a Stage 4 downtrend to rally for 1 to 3 days right back into its declining 8, 21, or 50 EMA.

Once price touches that daily moving average, drop down to the 30-minute chart:

- Wait for upward momentum to stall and form a 30m pivot low
- Enter when price breaks below that 30m candle low
- Put your stop right above the rejection high

That lets you trade the magnitude of a DAILY downtrend while defining your risk off a 30-minute candle.

And I manage shorts the exact same way I manage longs:

- Trim 25% into the first major support level or at 2R
- Move my stop to breakeven to finance the risk
- Trim another 25% into the next support zone
- Trail the remaining 50% using daily closes back above the 8 and 21 EMAs

Don't fight the tape.

When the market is above rising 8/21/50 EMAs, press leading longs.

When the market is below declining 8/21/50 EMAs, protect capital or trade weak names rejecting key levels.

6 PLAYBOOKS FOR 6 DIFFERENT MARKET REGIMES

Last week’s firm-wide @smbcapital meeting was about better adapting to different market regimes.

Which begs the question - what types of market regimes are there for discretionary traders? And what playbooks should we be running for each of them?

I’ll start and you all can expand on this in the replies.

Obviously this is far from exhaustive and kept high-level, but I think this should lead serious traders to do some deep reflection of their own.

1. Theme-driven bullish market. Markets like this one favor momentum strategies, swing longs, overnight momentum, IPOs, and headlines relevant to the driving theme.

Early summer of 2026 certainly fell under this with AI as the driver and leading sectors like DRAM / cloud computing.

2. Macro-driven markets. Think in the spring during the start of the U.S.-Iran conflict, think 2025 Trump tariffs and the Russia-Ukraine war. Due to the randomness and volatility of these headlines, this market highly favors breaking news trading as well as trading move-to-move, while greatly reducing overnight exposure and swing exposure.

3. Range-bound market. Think of the last couple months, where we've had no prevailing theme and most indices have stayed range-bound while the leaders from the prior cycle corrected. In this market, you want to reduce overall trading activity and risk allocation. If volatility is inflated, you might focus on selling vol, but you need to be careful of complacency. There can still be niche opportunities, but overall, most traders will find it best to be more hands-off.

4. Panic-driven markets. During these markets, breakdowns can work exceptionally well. However, I found that my greatest PNL, ironically, comes from longs in names that have totally capitulated, especially trading the right side of the V. Overnights and swing trades can again be quite favorable in this market. IPOs and headlines tend to take a back seat.

5. Idiosyncratic single-stock or single-sector moves. This is the type of market where broader themes are quiet, yet single stock names occupy the majority of your focus. This is where all risk should be allocated to the single stock or sector. I'm thinking of when we saw the epic short squeeze in $CAR this year, or prior moves in names like $MSTR, $GME, $AMC, or when sectors offer great opportunities, like the quantum names over the last few years.

6. Euphoria. We've seen markets like this in 2021 following COVID, as well as glimpses of this at times during the last couple years. In this market, small caps are on fire. Anything with a theme works, and momentum breakouts go so much further than you could have imagined. This is when swing trading and big-picture imagination work better than you could have ever imagined. Those that are quick to short or skeptical either miss out or risk ruin, as using your brain can get you killed here.

Further questions from here:
1. How do you identify each of these regimes FOR YOU? For example, one criteria might be a certain vix level or sentiment metric.
2. What playbooks are you competent in for each of those?
3. How much risk do each of these markets deserve?

系统性分析一下Elon Mask对于neocloud的负面评价

首先，市场真正会惩罚的是那些有债务在身拼命扩张同时无法履行合约的Neocloud公司

这里的无法履行合约是指违反条款是指：

Termination for cause (因违约终止合同)：到了最后期限还是没能交付算力或长期无法达到运行标准

这会造成严重后果不限于：

Cross-default （连带的债务违约）：只要一个债务违约其他所有债务全部违约，所有债主同时讨债，对现金流造成致命性的打击

Cover Costs （替代成本赔偿）：当你无法按时交付算力，客户去另一个算力出售商购买所造成的额外加差由你来补，对你的现金流造成额外的压力

当然，马斯克在文中明确暗示200+ neoclouds are going to see supply-level death..

并不是所有的neoclouds都会陷入他所描述的危机，世界上有数以百计的neoclouds的公司；当然，如果马斯克所描述的事情真实发生，NBIS不会是one of them

IREN在用业内所有人都看重的现金流去和皇马，NBA合作打广告；Corewave债务规模十分之大 并且其大规模基建在美国境内 同时目前中期选举前后已经有各个州开始反对数据中心建设（之前我中期选举分析预测贴已经讲过）

反观NBIS在做什么，他在规避任何额外融资的scenario，同时在一直用较为保守的方式去保护自己的资产负债表

"From last week, probably some of the capital sources are temporarily closed, like asset backed or debt market, but we're working now with customer advances and several other instruments which will probably provide us enough finance to build this gigawatt at least."（从上周开始，资产抵押和债务市场这类融资渠道暂时关上了，但我们在用客户预付款和其他几种工具，应该足够建下一个 1GW。）

系统性分析一下Elon Mask对于neocloud的负面评价

首先，市场真正会惩罚的是那些有债务在身拼命扩张同时无法履行合约的Neocloud公司

这里的无法履行合约是指违反条款是指：

Termination for cause (因违约终止合同)：到了最后期限还是没能交付算力或长期无法达到运行标准

这会造成严重后果不限于：

Cross-default （连带的债务违约）：只要一个债务违约其他所有债务全部违约，所有债主同时讨债，对现金流造成致命性的打击

Cover Costs （替代成本赔偿）：当你无法按时交付算力，客户去另一个算力出售商购买所造成的额外加差由你来补，对你的现金流造成额外的压力

当然，马斯克在文中明确暗示200+ neoclouds are going to see supply-level death..

并不是所有的neoclouds都会陷入他所描述的危机，世界上有数以百计的neoclouds的公司；当然，如果马斯克所描述的事情真实发生，NBIS不会是one of them

IREN在用业内所有人都看重的现金流去和皇马，NBA合作打广告；Corewave债务规模十分之大 并且其大规模基建在美国境内 同时目前中期选举前后已经有各个州开始反对数据中心建设（之前我中期选举分析预测贴已经讲过）

"We have a pretty healthy balance sheet. We have always been more conservative here."（我们的资产负债表很健康，一直比较保守。）

"From last week, probably some of the capital sources are temporarily closed, like asset backed or debt market, but we're working now with customer advances and several other instruments which will probably provide us enough finance to build this gigawatt at least."（从上周开始，资产抵押和债务市场这类融资渠道暂时关上了，但我们在用客户预付款和其他几种工具，应该足够建下一个 1GW。）

被问到稳健的财务风格是不是来自 Yandex 的经验，他说："We're building a real company. A real company is measured by earnings... a healthy business is a business where we generate cash."（我们在建一家真正的公司，衡量标准是盈利，健康的企业能产生现金。）

核心担忧就是市场担心neocloud来了个有力竞争者，各neocloud之间会为了争抢出租自己的算力而降价。

我们如此可见，neocloud在市场眼里的死穴就是租金价格问题，因为这不仅仅关联了毛利率问题，同时又关联了股东能收到多少钱的问题。

我最新分析的Citadel的CEO格里芬对于目前AI板块最前沿的担忧就是明年也就是2027年是否会出现大批量过剩算力导致GPU租金下降 从而导致其板块被下杀严重。同时新一代芯片上线也会带来额外的设备折旧压力。

小公司倒下以后，它们的客户还需要算力，只能去找 NBIS 这类有交付能力的公司；一个neocloud的小公司离开所空出来的算力订单很小，也许都不够塞牙缝，但是十个呢？二十个呢？或者像elon讲的 200个呢？

它们集体被wiped out会给未来算力过剩问题留出很大的解法和空间，因此可以规避GPU租金降价的可能性...
这个推演是十分大概率发生的，它只有一个可能造成GPU租金降价的小漏洞就是如果小公司倒闭，它会有很多甩卖的折扣GPU要打包卖出

那么这个时候，一定会有Neocloud或者马斯克跑出来抢购或者被google meta这种公司自己打包买走训练自己的模型

而不是流向其余的竞争公司用来刻意压低GPU租金价格；目前，所有neocloud板块的公司应该都知道，如果当出现了GPU竞价的后果是什么

其后果不仅代表着公司毛利率降低还可能被金融市场揣测出是否demand跟不上了的情况

所以，选对公司，长期持有，并且时刻的关注基本面和相关情况变化

Where I was wrong $NVDA 🩸

A couple of weeks ago, I thought the $170 to $160 support level would hold.

It broke much faster than I expected.

Last week, I said that if $160 broke, my worst case was a move down toward $120. That’s where we are now.

The volume gap from about $175 down to about $130 has filled. Price closed Friday at $125.53, approaching my worst-case zone of $110 to $120.

The SEC report
On Friday, Reuters reported that the SEC’s enforcement staff is looking into potential accounting issues at NVIDIA, citing two unnamed people briefed on the matter.

The stock fell about 14% that day, wiping out roughly $491 billion in value.

Here’s what’s actually known, and what isn’t:
It’s a reported investigation, not charges.

No fraud allegations or charges have been made public.

Reuters couldn’t determine when the inquiry started or which accounting issues it covers.

NVIDIA didn’t immediately comment, and the SEC declined to comment.

As Reuters points out, investigations aren’t evidence of wrongdoing, and many close without any enforcement action.

We talked about this risk last week in my Weekly Watchlist writeup

Michael Burry flagged NVIDIA’s switch to reporting revenue by customer headquarters, which made Singapore disappear from its geographic breakdown.

And in my earnings checklist, I asked whether NVIDIA was helping fund the demand that buys its chips.

Reuters mentions both as background concerns, but it doesn’t say either one is part of the SEC’s inquiry.

On this week’s earnings call, interim CEO Colette Kress said NVIDIA would be “far more selective” about AI financing deals.

If it comes out that the company has done something wrong, this could get a lot worse.

Until then, it’s an open question, not a verdict.

Why $110 to $120 is my worst case
It’s the weekly smart money zone.

Measured from the $86 swing low, the zone sits right at $110 to $120.

It’s extremely oversold. Price is now well below the monthly smart money band, the strongest band we have, which holds about 85% of the time.

There’s a huge point of control at about $118.

The point of control is the price where the most shares have traded on the volume profile, so it tends to act as strong support.

In my opinion, price bottoms somewhere between here and about $110. That’s about 12% below Friday’s close.

What I expect short term
A relief rally is on the horizon. I think there’s a really good chance the short-term bottom forms between $120 and $125.

From there, a bounce of about 30% to 40%, back to $170, filling the volume profile.

Then one more short-term rejection at the weekly smart money band and the $170 resistance.

The bigger picture
I think we’re approaching a very long-term bottom. If I wanted to own NVIDIA for the next year, this is an insane place to do it.

But that doesn’t mean price bounces and runs back to all-time highs in a couple of weeks. I think a bounce is coming, and I think the bottom is forming. I’m just not expecting a push to all-time highs in the next couple of months.

昨天马斯克在警告，算力从掏钱买卡到机房通电、散热并稳定产生收入，全行业只要累计延迟几个月，明年就会出现数千亿美元的流动资金短缺。这其实从工程和资金周转的侧面，印证了我一直强调的核心判断：明年（2027 年）才是这批新兴算力租赁商真正交成绩单的时候，也是业绩与债务危机集中爆发的生死年。

很多朋友可能会问，像 CoreWeave（CRWV）这类小云现在不是已经接到微软、Meta、OpenAI 的大单、开始挣 AI 的钱了吗，为什么偏偏说明年才是真正的兑现期？把账拆开看就全明白了：现在它们收到的叫营收流水，不叫真正赚到手的净利润。

第一，为什么前两年没事，偏偏明年业绩才面临真正的兑现大考？

过去两年（2025 到 2026 年），小云正处在疯狂买卡和建数据中心的扩张期。那时候很多机房还在建设中，建设期的利息可以计入资产成本，华尔街盯着的也是积压订单和营收增速，容忍它们净利润亏损、自由现金流大幅为负。但到了明年，前期砸下数百亿美元采购的天量显卡陆续交付转固，会计规则瞬间变脸——巨额的设备折旧费（通常按四五年摊销）和贷款利息，全部正式计入当期利润表硬扣。这时候再也不能靠未来订单画饼了，收上来的算力租金扣除天价折旧、电费运维和高额利息之后，到底能不能剩下实打实的净利润，明年财报必须真刀真枪交卷。

第二，为什么偏偏明年债务和外部环境会形成致命双杀？

小云没有亚马逊、微软、谷歌那种庞大的广告和传统云业务源源不断输血，买显卡全靠拿 GPU 做抵押借年化十几个点的高息私募信贷。前两年借的大量两三年期高息债务，正好在明年集中进入还本付息高峰。偏偏到了明年，一方面大客户们自己的超级数据中心和自研芯片陆续上线，小云享受的算力紧缺高溢价红利正在消退，续约议价能力大打折扣；另一方面现在美股 AI 板块剧烈杀估值、信贷市场全面收紧，华尔街已经不再为纯扩张故事买单，借新还旧的路彻底堵死。

把这两条线合在一起，明年小云面对的是一场两头夹击的生死局：一头是交付延迟带来的流动资金黑洞，一头是天量折旧压顶与高息债务集中到期。当行业从拼胆量借钱囤卡进入拼盈利、运营和偿债能力的下半场，缺乏自我造血能力的纯算力二道贩子，明年免不了一场残酷的大洗牌，所以我也一直在说，小云风险太大，我只看大云……

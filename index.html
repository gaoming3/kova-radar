("AST SpaceMobile") (NASDAQ: ASTS), the company building the first and only space-based cellular broadband network accessible directly by everyday smartphones, designed for both commercial and government applications, today announced the successful orbital launch of its BlueBird satellites 11, 12, and 13.

The mission lifted off at 3:42 a.m. EDT on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station aboard a Falcon 9 rocket, expanding AST SpaceMobile’s growing constellation of next-generation BlueBird satellites following the successful launch and deployment of BlueBirds 8, 9, and 10. With BlueBirds 14, 15, and 16 preparing for the next mission and production advancing through BlueBird satellite 42, the company continues to execute its constellation deployment program at scale.

BlueBird satellites 11, 12, and 13 are the largest communications arrays ever deployed in low Earth orbit, more than three times the size of the company’s five initial Block 1 BlueBird satellites. The large apertures and advanced beamforming capabilities enable greater targeted coverage, reduced interference, and increased capacity, supporting reliable cellular broadband connectivity directly to standard, unmodified smartphones. These next-generation satellites are expected to deliver peak data rates approaching 200 Mbps, nearly double the 98.9 Mbps achieved by the company's five initial Block 1 BlueBird satellites, supporting voice, broadband data, and video applications from space.

Our unique technology is designed to provide broadband for direct-to-device but is increasingly serving as a platform for additional capabilities. We remain focused on scaling our cellular broadband network as we prepare for beta services later this year.”

Each launch advances the company’s goal of delivering seamless, space-based cellular broadband integrated with terrestrial mobile networks, leveraging a flexible spectrum strategy across both partner and AST SpaceMobile spectrum, together with standard 4G and 5G devices.

As the constellation grows, the company progresses toward initial service activation and continuous coverage across key markets including the United States, Canada, Europe, Saudi Arabia, and Japan, as well as the U.S. Government and other strategic markets.

AST SpaceMobile has agreements with nearly 60 mobile network operators globally representing more than 3 billion subscribers combined, together with strategic partnerships with AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, Rakuten, Google, Bell, Telus, stc Group, and American Tower.

BlueBird satellites are assembled, integrated, and tested at AST SpaceMobile’s facilities in Midland Texas, reflecting the company’s commitment to U.S.-based innovation and manufacturing. AST SpaceMobile now operates over 500,000 square feet of manufacturing and operations facilities worldwide, supported by a 2,250+ workforce, and a technology platform backed by over 3,900 patents and patent-pending claims.

SpaceX上市后的首份财报其实还可以的。
二季度营收达到78.14亿美元，同比增长92%；每股亏损0.09美元，净亏损5.41亿美元，均好于财报前的市场预期。

吓到市场的是资本开支。

AMD：全面超预期，依旧不够
AMD的财报同样不能算差。
二季度营收115.36亿美元，同比增长50%；调整后每股收益1.66美元。数据中心收入达到67亿美元，同比增长107%，已经占公司总收入的58%。公司给出的三季度营收指引约为130亿美元，也高于市场此前约125亿美元的预期。
问题在于，AMD财报前已经上涨了7%，市场交易的是：Helios整机系统能否带来更陡峭的增长曲线，AMD能否明显抢走英伟达的AI份额，以及利润率能否同步加速。

结果公司给出的三季度非GAAP毛利率预期仍为56%，没有继续明显扩张。对于一家已经被按照AI核心资产定价的公司来说，仅仅超过分析师预测还不够，它需要不断超过最乐观资金的想象。
所以AMD的盘后暴跌，不是在否定基本面，而是在重新校准估值：业绩很好，但财报前的价格已经提前交易了更好的业绩。

吓到市场的是资本开支。

AMD：全面超预期，依旧不够
AMD的财报同样不能算差。
二季度营收115.36亿美元，同比增长50%；调整后每股收益1.66美元。数据中心收入达到67亿美元，同比增长107%，已经占公司总收入的58%。公司给出的三季度营收指引约为130亿美元，也高于市场此前约125亿美元的预期。

吓到市场的是资本开支。
SpaceX单季度资本开支高达183.69亿美元，其中AI业务就投入了158.28亿美元，远远超过当期总营收。虽然AI业务收入同比增长247%，调整后EBITDA也首次转正，但这种增长是靠极其激进的算力建设换来的。

问题在于，AMD财报前已经上涨了7%，市场交易的是：Helios整机系统能否带来更陡峭的增长曲线，AMD能否明显抢走英伟达的AI份额，以及利润率能否同步加速。

所以AMD的盘后暴跌，不是在否定基本面，而是在重新校准估值：业绩很好，但财报前的价格已经提前交易了更好的业绩。

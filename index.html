Micron next week

With earnings next week likely to underscore Micron’s AI-driven earnings power, MU is trading at less than 7× fiscal-2027 consensus earnings—a valuation that still treats memory as a purely cyclical commodity business. The market is pricing little durability into an earnings base being reshaped by HBM, advanced DRAM, and a far more concentrated industry structure.

The central question is whether Micron can finally earn a structural rerating. Memory will never lose its cyclical character, but the investment case no longer rests on commodity DRAM alone. HBM is a technically demanding, supply-constrained product with high qualification barriers, deep customer integration, and a small set of credible suppliers. If Micron can demonstrate that its AI-memory mix supports consistently higher margins and returns through the cycle, a single-digit earnings multiple will look increasingly untenable.

The market does not need to treat MU like Nvidia; it only needs to stop valuing a strategically essential AI infrastructure supplier as if it were an undifferentiated commodity producer.

但 @Backpack 这回做的，不太是同一个东西。

它把「真实美股持仓」和「链上交易账户」塞进了同一个统一保证金池。股票不用卖，就能在同一个钱包里借 USD、跑永续、做现货保证金——而且这些仓位共用一份抵押，不像别家非得把现货、期货、保证金几个账户拆开摆。

这点区别看着小，其实挺关键。

底层是因为它手里的股票是「真股权」：每一股对应美国托管里的实股，按 UCC 第八条算证券权益，走 ACATS/DTCC 那套清算托管。不是拿价格做个合成敞口就算了。

更妙的是，这些股票还能在 Solana 上代币化——自己托管、7×24 交易、能直接接 DeFi。也就是说，押在保证金系统里的那批持仓，本质是个链上原语，能伸到更宽的 Solana 生态里去，而不只是券商账本上的一行数字。

这层传统券商复制不了。

能当抵押的标的，已经从最早的 MU、SNDK 扩到 17 个：TSLA、NVDA、AAPL、GOOGL、META、AMZN、AMD 都在名单里。量也起来了——Backpack 一家就喂出了 Solana 代币化股票 30 天 33 亿美元成交量里的 11 亿，占三分之一；$GRND 上线两小时干了 1410 万，比它在纽交所当天的量还高出三倍。

所以这事真正该问的，不是「我能不能拿特斯拉股票借点钱」。

而是：一个把持牌经纪商和链上保证金引擎揉进同一个钱包的账户，会不会就是下一代金融账户该有的样子。

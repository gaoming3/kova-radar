$ASTS: 🚨 DEUTSCHE BANK UPGRADES AST SPACEMOBILE TO BUY FROM HOLD WITH $93 PRICE TARGET

Upgrading to Buy
We are upgrading ASTS to Buy and maintaining our $93 PT. The stock is down ~65% from its all-time high in May. We had downgraded the stock in May after the New Glenn launchpad explosion that introduced a material delay in ASTS' launch plans, combined with valuation concerns. However, we believe now is an opportune time to become more constructive given the recent stock-price weakness, a number of positive developments/catalysts that we think have gone under the radar, and spectrum value that we estimate now comprises all but $10 per share in ASTS' EV. Furthermore, we think the -10% move on Friday was misguided since the 800 MHz spectrum SpaceX will be acquiring doesn't make SpaceX more competitive with ASTS as an MNO partner for D2D, and ASTS isn't targeting direct-to-consumer (for which SpaceX will primarily use the 800 MHz).

We see a number of positive developments/catalysts for ASTS in the near term:

➡️ We believe it is increasingly likely that T-Mobile will switch from Starlink to ASTS as its D2D partner (through the JV). Roughly coincident with the timing of the announcement that AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile reached a definitive agreement to form a D2D JV on Oct. 1; T-Mobile appears to have removed many (but not all) references to Starlink from its website. Furthermore, SpaceX's efforts to compete with the Big 3 MNOs in mobile continues to antagonize the Big 3 (and shrink their market capitalizations), pushing them even closer to ASTS. SpaceX's announced purchase of 14 MHz of 800 MHz spectrum from Grain Management on October 8 was the latest shot across the bows of the Big 3, leading to a 10-13% decline in their stock prices on Friday (just the latest SpaceX-driven market-cap destruction for the telcos).

➡️ The Space Development Agency (SDA) sent a letter to the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (to be then forwarded to the FCC) in September expressing support for ASTS' request for Temporary Authority to conduct testing of pulsed radiolocation capabilities in the 902–928 MHz band using its in-orbit BlueBird satellites. The letter names ASTS as a subcontractor and stresses the national security importance of testing these capabilities (which appear to be sensing related). We believe this is indicative of continued progress by ASTS in expanding its government contract base, a revenue opportunity that seems to be expanding every day.

➡️ The preliminary selection of ASTS (along with Rakuten) for the Japan LEO project took a step forward when the October 5 Joint Statement on the U.S.-Japan Technology Prosperity Deal specifically "welcomed Japan's Rakuten Group and the U.S.-based AST SpaceMobile cooperation for the development and deployment of Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite infrastructure". We view this as a sign that the official J-LEO authorization could be coming soon. If/when approved, it means the Government of Japan will fund up to $1B of capex for ASTS' satellite constellation in exchange for sovereign use and control of a number of satellites while they are able to provide coverage to Japan. This "up to $1B" is not reflected in our current valuation, even though it would represent essentially free capital, significantly improving ASTS' liquidity position.

Underlying Spectrum Value Means The Value of The Business Itself Is Highly Discounted

ASTS' EV is currently $23B, while we estimate the spectrum rights ASTS has through its long-term lease with Ligado represent $19B in value, implying only a $4B valuation for the rest of the company, or $10 per share. This assumes $1.09 per MHz*POP, equal to the price SpaceX agreed to pay Echostar in that transaction signed in September 2025.

We Also See Optionality From Potential Industry Consolidation

We believe ASTS could be of significant strategic value to a larger company with LEO and D2D ambitions given its spectrum position, novel satellite architecture, IP/patent portfolio, spectrum rights, MNO partnerships, engineering talent, manufacturing capabilities/facilities, and nascent government business.

Launch Is Still A Challenge, But Looks Incrementally Better Today

Our view has been for some time that securing launch and putting satellites into orbit is the most critical success factor for ASTS over the next couple years because it would facilitate commercial-service introduction and begin to accelerate revenue growth. It is also important defensively to ensure MNO partners stick with ASTS and the company's government-contract base expands. The launch bottleneck continues to be a challenge. However, Blue Origin seems to be making encouraging progress toward rebuilding the launch pad destroyed earlier this year (potentially launching again before YE), and ASTS is prepared to pay a premium for additional launch from SpaceX and others to help mitigate the impact of less Blue Origin launch availability. Thus, while we continue to have concerns regarding launch availability; (1) we think the picture is incrementally positive relative to a quarter ago given Blue Origin's progress, and (2) we think these other aforementioned catalysts will define the narrative between now and the end of the year, since the industry launch-capacity situation is already fairly well appreciated by the market.

小資金如何快速做大？

理論上說，小資金要做大，必須要採用「重倉+槓桿」的操作才行。然而，這種方式如果用在了錯誤的標的和錯誤的方向上，必定是歸零。

所以，我的看法是：
1. 不要碰個股，只做美股大盤指數，比如納斯達克100指數 。它不會跌到零，它的優勝劣汰機制決定了指數長期上漲是必然的；
2. 在長期必然上漲的標的上「重倉+槓桿」做多，在概率上是有利的。

具體怎麼做？我提供一個思路供參考：
1. 在上升趨勢中（請注意這個前提），每次納斯達克100指數從前一個高點回撤超過-3%，收盤前買入 $QQQ 看漲期權；
2. 選擇到期日>90天、價外5%左右的合約；
3. 期權增值100%止盈，虧損-30%止損出局。

簡言之，用3:1的盈虧比，在長期必然上漲的標的上「重倉+槓桿」，長期堅持這麼做，你的資本會飛速增長。

增長到一定程度（比如每增長10萬美元）就將帳戶資金的80%買入 $VOO ，剩餘20%繼續以小搏大。如此往復，這是最容易讓小資金迅速放大的方式。

當然，這並非沒有風險。 $QQQ 在2000年科技泡沫崩盤後，用了十六年才創出新高，在這期間，不論怎麼做都難以獲利。

說到底，我們多數時候賺的都是趨勢的錢。所以，研究趨勢是我的主要工作，感興趣的話可以來看看我的交易筆記，每個交易日更新。

JPMorgan Resumes Coverage on $SNDK with Overweight Rating, PT $2,250

Analyst comments: “In our view, Sandisk is in many respects uniquely positioned to capture the ongoing structural inflection in NAND demand driven by rapid growth in AI inference, with three key dynamics underpinning our OW thesis:

1) the New Business Model (NBM) framework (or LTAs - long-term agreements) has structurally reset SNDK’s margin profile higher and materially reduced cyclicality, with 8 signed NBMs representing ~$94B in total contract value at floor pricing, weighted-average contract duration of 4+ years, and ~80% GM even at the floor pricing tier;

2) NAND market TAM is inflecting from ~$70B in CY25 (JPMe) to $300B+ in CY26 and ~$500B in CY27, with Data Center representing the largest driver of growth (by far) as hyperscale/cloud customers increasingly deploy flash for inference-era workloads (incl. persistent KV cache - a major driver of bit demand growth); and

3) SNDK is delivering technology leadership, with BiCS10 sampling ahead of schedule (332-layer 2Tb QLC with 65% more bits per wafer than BiCS8), a projected 27% CAGR in per-wafer bit productivity through BiCS generations, and High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) emerging as a differentiated inference-era memory platform.

Management introduced a long-term financial model for FY28-FY30 targeting mid-/high-teens% revenue growth (in line with bit growth - implying ~flat pricing was conservatively assumed), ~80% GM, ~75% OpM, and ~50% adjusted FCF margin, representing a clear step-change from historical norms where GMs oscillated well below this level.

To us, this screens as a substantive reset rather than peak-cycle pricing capture, with NBMs accounting for >50% of FY27 bits and growing to ~2/3 in FY28, and contracts detailing month-by-month volume, mix, and price commitments backed by $16.5B in financial guarantees, significantly improving forward visibility from the ‘three months of pricing visibility’ that has been ‘standard operating procedure’ for the industry to 4+ years of committed economics with strategic customers.

Even at floor pricing, we think NBMs sustain ~80% GMs across the multi-year contract lives, with upside captured through variable pricing mechanisms. We also view the LT financial model as embedding meaningful earnings leverage that we think is not fully captured in current consensus estimates, as mid-/high-teens% revenue growth ultimately translates into an EPS growth CAGR north of 25%. Layered on top, management's commitment to returning 100% of excess cash flow to shareholders should drive an aggressive share count reduction trajectory that further amplifies EPS growth on a per-share basis.

We think this combination of higher through-cycle profitability, sustained bit demand growth, and share count reduction sets up a compounding earnings power dynamic that supports our CY27 EPS estimate of $250 and, in our view, points to a multi-year path toward meaningfully higher EPS levels beyond CY27. Longer-term, we view HBF and emerging 3D Matrix Memory technology as meaningful virtual call options on inference-era memory architecture that, in our view, are not fully reflected in current consensus estimates or valuation.”

Analyst: Harlan Sur

The story today about Silver Lake, a premier private equity firm with over $100B in assets focused on tech, potentially looking to acquire $WDAY likely puts a floor under software sector for some time.

Workday prior to today’s 18% move higher had been down 18% over the past year (8/12/25-8/12/26) vs $IGV (the software ETF) which was down 6% and the S&P up 20%. It was considered one of the names most likely to be disintermediated by AI.

PE firms 1) use debt which increases risk if they are wrong, 2) the cost of longer-term debt is the highest in nearly 20 years and 3) they need to feel comfortable about terminal value given exits are typically now in 5-7 years. This is a much harder bar to clear than a typical public market investor who has daily liquidity if they change their mind or a strategic acquirer that may have other synergistic reasons to do the deal.

Also the size of this deal makes the bar even harder to clear. With a closing market cap today of over $50B for Workday, if it were to occur this would rival the prior record breaking technology PE deal for Electronic Arts of ~$55B that Silver Lake was also involved in.

This news follows the results from $TEAM last week, another name that investors have put in the AI disintermediation basket, which rallied 35% the next day in reaction to earnings and is still down 2% from 8/12/25-8/12/26.

I continue to believe that AI native companies like Anthropic and OpenAI which I believe are now likely running at over $100B in annualized revenue run rate combined vs $29B to start the year will increasingly put under pressure: 1) point solution software companies that are not system of record, security or gaming, 2) headcount growth as AI empowers current employees and 3) IT services vendors. Every public company will need to find cost savings somewhere given their rapid ramp in AI spending if they want to make Wall Street forecasts.

Having said that, these two recent events should help put a floor under the software sector as a whole and may help it to outperform between now and year-end given the massive underperformance and multiple compression seen since it peaked in October of last year.

但今天最狠的一句话,不是数字,是 "100% 返还"。

内存行业过去的死法是固定剧本:赚到超额利润 → 全砸进扩产 → 三年后供给过剩 → 大家一起跌穿成本线。周期不是天气,是行业自己用超额利润浇出来的。现在管理层公开承诺:超额现金一分不留,全还股东——等于当着全行业的面宣布,我不拿这笔钱去扩产了。 这不只是股东回报,这是供给纪律的信号弹。默契不需要密室,Investor Day 就是信号塔。

而 Sandisk 是三大存储玩家里体量最小的那个。最小的先把剧本打完整了:周期改合同、利润改返还。已经有人在推演下一步——若美光、海力士、三星照抄,三巨头未来三年的自由现金流合计可能是万亿美元量级,全部对着回购和分红去。卷产能的行业,切换成卷回报的行业,这是操作系统级的更换。

从怀疑到改票,用了八天和一本 $939 亿的合同簿。

周期没有死于需求,它死于合同。Figures never lie——这次连地板价都写成白纸黑字了。

8 月 13 号，$TSLA 的波动率掉到52周最低，换手率1%。

说实话，看到这个数据我也是无语了。

要知道这两三年，特斯拉的波动率在个股里一直是数一数二的高——尤其财报前后，还有那几次特殊时刻：川普当选、马斯克跟川普闹掰。哪次都是波动率爆表时刻。

因为马斯克老马同志，心直口快、推特上说话没个把门的，股价整天坐飞机，人送外号七大权重唯一的妖股。所以特斯拉一直是散户和期权投资者的心头爱。

现在居然安静成这样。

我猜有两个原因：
一是 SpaceX 在前面吸走了火力，大家的注意力全跑那边去了。
二是这些年 FSD 虽然没马斯克吹得那么快，但进展是肉眼可见的，空头在这个价位也兴趣不大了。

但波动率这东西，跟股价一样，是会均值回归的。
而且低波动本身就会制造高波动：

一个自我循环。

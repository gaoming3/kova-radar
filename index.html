Or I can move my stop to breakeven, give the position some room, and play it for a potentially much bigger move, having open-ended profit potential with zero risk.
That's exactly where I am now. The profit I've built allowed me to move my stop to breakeven and effectively eliminate the original risk. I've earned the right to play for the bigger move, working my way into the best situation possible; a profitable freeroll.
And that's the key. I don't enter a trade and immediately decide that I'm going to hold out for some pre-determined target or enormous move regardless of what happens. First, the trade has to prove itself. If it moves sufficiently in my favor and gives me an opportunity to de-risk the position, then I can decide whether to take the profit or give it time to develop into something more.
That's how you put yourself in position for asymmetric leverage: start with small risk, let the trade earn its way, improve your worst-case scenario, eliminate the risk when the opportunity presents itself, and then give the bigger move a chance to happen risk free.
Of course, at that point, you always have the option of taking a 5R profit, and that in itself can make you rich.

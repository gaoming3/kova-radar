BSCN@BSCNews12h
Pi Network Is on the Open USD Partners List. Why Does That Matter?

Open USD ($OUSD) is a dollar coin controlled by Open Standard (@openstandard).

Open Standard is an independent corporation with a large network of partners, including banks, payments companies, tech firms, and crypto companies.⁠

Pi Network (@PiCoreTeam) is now listed on this official partners page.

Being listed doesn’t mean that a product is launched. It means the project becomes part of the larger dialogue about dollar rail collaboration.

(1) Recognition alongside familiar names

The directory comprises payment providers, banks, asset managers, and cryptocurrency companies.

Being listed among them makes $Pi part of discussions usually reserved for organizations already transmitting payments in large volumes.

(2) A unified USD standard

Open USD has been developed to let enterprises mint and exchange their shares without charges or volume limits, while most earnings are reinvested to partners, with only an operating cost taken out.

For the network focused on ordinary people, such a structure could, in theory, provide settlement options without relying solely on an unstable native currency.

(3) Decentralized governance

The Open Standard project is organized so participating companies take part in both economic and governance processes, rather than leaving all control to one issuer.

(4) What the listing does not establish

The listing does not prove that Open USD is active on Pi, that one can now spend the currency inside the app, or that Pi’s developers have announced a final commercial deal.

(5) Why people are paying attention

For communities, it is an opportunity to realize payment functionality and international commerce.

For observers, it is a case study in whether large user bases can integrate themselves into institutional stablecoin plumbing.

Partner listings are an initial step in the process.

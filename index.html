a staking reward can have more than one destination, and @PlayOnMint makes that choice fairly important

claim takes the $MNTD reward out of the staking position, leaving the original stake in place while the earned tokens become available separately

compound does the opposite

the reward goes back into the stake, increasing the amount that remains inside the position and potentially contributing to further Status progression

so the choice is really about what happens to the reward after it is earned

claim keeps it outside the position

compound keeps it working inside the position

that is also why i would separate both options from the lock feature

locking changes how easily the underlying stake can be unstaked

claim and compound decide where the reward goes

those are different decisions, even though they sit within the same staking system

there is another detail worth keeping in mind

https://t.co/ZlSvmnMCW9 still marks some levels, rates and figures as placeholders until launch, so the final live terms are more important than assuming the current numbers are fixed

the useful way to read the setup is therefore simple

claim changes where the reward sits

compound changes the balance being staked

lock changes the flexibility around the stake

three mechanics, three different effects

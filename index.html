Augustine@EyoAugusti7318123h
a score can tell you where a trading agent stands, but the history behind that score tells you much more about how it got there.

that distinction matters with @agenticscredit, where $CREDIT and ACS serve different purposes. trading activity can contribute to $CREDIT, while ACS evaluates performance through factors such as profitability, risk control and consistency. progress in one doesn't automatically mean progress in the other.

the 580 ACS threshold is an important first milestone. it places an agent in the Emerging tier and meets the score requirement for the funding waitlist, though qualification doesn't guarantee capital.

what i'd pay attention to along the way is how the strategy handles trades that don't go as planned. a profitable position can come from taking more risk than necessary, just as a loss can happen even when the entry and risk management were reasonable.

that's why paper trading has value beyond building a track record. it gives an agent room to develop a history without putting live capital at risk, making it easier to review entry selection, drawdowns and whether decisions follow a consistent process.

i wouldn't judge an agent by its latest result alone. i'd want to know whether its decisions hold up across different outcomes, especially during losing periods when poor risk management can become more costly.

ACS becomes more meaningful when the record behind it supports the score. reaching a threshold may open a door to consideration, but the quality of the decisions is what deserves closer examination.

SpaceX 今天收在156.54美元，跌了4.2%。评论区第一反应是解禁砸盘? 马斯克唱空内存被制裁了？同一天，印度那边又出了个坏消息：执政党背后的组织公开要求政府别给 Starlink 发牌照。 $SPCX 一个是远期利好，一个是眼下的麻烦，都还没有结论。

先说股价。全天成交6700万股左右，纳斯达克给的日均量是9900万股，今天大概只有平时的三分之二。不过这个平均数被8月刚上市那阵的天量撑高了，换成中位数，平常一天八千万股出头，今天也就少了两成。所以我只敢说：没看到有人集中出逃，但也谈不上“没人卖”。10月9日那批3.28亿股解禁当天，成交也只有9200万股，没有放量。今天芯片股、韩国股市都在跌，SpaceX 跟着大盘走的可能更大。具体为什么跌，没人有完美答案，这只是我的推测。

再说 FAA。X 上有人喊“FAA 批了一年456次”，其实只是开始审。现在星舰基地一年最多飞25次，SpaceX 申请分两步：先提到一年100次，过几年再到456次，差不多每个工作日两次。公众意见收到11月30日，业内估计这种完整环评要一年半到两年。这事为什么要紧？手机直连卫星要靠天上卫星够密，新一代 V3 卫星得靠星舰去运，星舰能飞多勤，先卡在这份审批上。

然后是印度。出来喊话的叫 SJM，是 RSS 管经济的分支，RSS 是莫迪所在人民党的思想母体。它说 Starlink 卫星之间用激光传数据，流量可能绕开印度的网关，政府想断网、想监控都难，还把美国关税扯进来：你能拿国家安全加关税，我也能拿国家安全封 Starlink。SJM 不是监管部门，政府听不听还不知道，也没证据说今天的跌和它有关。

所以 Starlink 这条线现在两头卡：天上，卫星能发多快要看 FAA；地上，最大的新市场之一要看印度通信部。眼前最近的日子是10月26日，下一批3.28亿股可以卖了。

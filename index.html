GOOD MORNING & WELCOME TO FOMC DECISION WEEK.

In addition to a potential rate hike, the fears over the weekend caused by Dario’s essay have led to a long software/short semis premarket.

Nvidia, Marvell, Sandisk, AMD, Nebius, Micron, CoreWeave, Intel are all red from 3-7%.

Palantir, Atlassian, Crowdstrike, Service Now, Adobe, Intuit are all green from 2-5%.

Personally, I think the market is having a knee jerk reaction to the headlines over the weekend.

In the past 24 hours, Anthropic themselves have signed multiple new compute deals (one with Rumble for $14B yesterday) and are preparing for their IPO by officially picking the Nasdaq as their exchange. In the face of all the “pace the frontier” slowdown news, the company creating that news is not slowing down their compute obligations.

So why is the market reacting like this? Well, the entire AI trade is rooted in capex. Until there is more clarity on what it means to slow down model training and recursive learning, the market is assuming that means there is a closer end to the growth in capex than expected which obviously is bad for the AI names. Great for software as more money gets allocated to the application layer and great for hyperscalers since they can generate more FCF which is why those names are green premarket.

I think this fear could flip within days if not within hours, but if you match this fear with a rate hike that might be priced in but still is not good for high-beta growth (which is what the AI trade is composed of) then you start to see how the market can make sense of taking these names down further.

Jensen speaks at the All-In Summit today, he’s likely either going to completely reject Sam/Dario’s fear-inducing message by giving a more optimistic approach to think about AI or agree with it and still make the claim that compute obligations, even if not needed to train new models as fast as possible, are not slowing down.

Going to be a big week.

美元↑
油价↑
纳指期货↓

昨天60%，今天突然变35%，为什么？

9/1，今天半导体集体下杀，很多人问：AI 是不是要暂缓，甚至暂停了？很多好股票 例如 $MRVL 跌到这个位置，是不是可以下手？

1/ 我认为今天主要有三股力量叠在一起：
> 原油供应再出问题，油价上涨
> 加息预期与长期国债收益率上升
> 周末前沿 AI 限速讨论，让市场重新考虑未来几年的扩建速度

2/ 我的理解是，市场同时在调整不同公司的前景：前沿模型推进变慢，激进扩建的回报可能推迟；传统软件被快速替代的担忧可能缓和；AI 安全风险受到重视，又可能提高网络安全支出的优先级

举个简化例子。客户现在有 100 单位算力，原来准备扩到 200，需要新增采购 100。后来扩建目标改成 160，只需要新增采购 60
客户的算力仍然增长了 60%，供应商面对的新增采购却比原计划少了 40%

所以，AI 越来越普及，与某一年的硬件收入低于市场原先期待，完全可以同时发生

3/ 那么，我们以 Marvell 为例，220 美元的 MRVL，到底要求公司做到什么？

今天下跌之后（见附图）
FY2028：收入增长约 51%，每股盈利增长约 60%。
FY2029：收入再增长约 44%，每股盈利再增长约 54%

做一个更具体的压力测试：
假设 FY2028 收入增长从约 51% 放慢到 30%（我觉得不太可能），收入约 157 亿美元；调整后净利率仍有 34%，稀释股数为 9.25 亿股，那么 EPS 约 5.76 美元

公司盈利仍比 FY2027 的 4.21 美元增长约 37%。但如果市场给 30 倍，价格只有约 173 美元，比 220 美元低约 21%

反过来，假设两年后市场仍相信 FY2029 每股盈利可以达到 10 美元（符合上次财报后的分析师预测）：
给 30 倍，对应 300 美元，从 220 美元买入的两年年化回报约 17%
给 25 倍，对应 250 美元，两年年化回报约 7%

因此，现在是否买入，我现在真正会问自己的核心问题是：最差的情况下即使油价和利率暂时降不下来，前沿训练也比此前设想推进得慢，MRVL 能否依靠互联升级和定制项目，按期做到约 7 美元、再往 10 美元走的每股盈利？

以及我是否愿意去下注次差或者更好的情况，我个人的答案是，肯定的

接下来改变我判断的，是客户采购和交付节奏，以及 10 月 6 日 MRVL 投资者日能否进一步支持 180 亿美元收入展望

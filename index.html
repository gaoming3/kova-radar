I’ve been extremely busy today so just getting round to gathering my thoughts on $ONDS Q3 earnings.

The top line numbers are great. Record revenues, EPS beat, raising FY26 guidance, $1.1B in FY27 guidance - just, simply insane numbers. Right?

But you have to look under the hood.

Let’s get into it.

Firstly, I’m disappointed that there wasn’t any (or limited) coverage on the “system-of-systems” approach. To lean so hard into it one quarter and then go back to covering only individual platforms felt strange.

There was a small mention of Iron Wave in the Q&A about that being deployed and receiving good feedback. And we know from Roboteam’s website that those deployment numbers are in the hundreds.

So why not make this a key point?

The whole thesis is that Ondas is acquiring and fusing these singular products together into these “SoS” platforms (like Iron Wave), so surely this is the validation of that thesis?

I’m also disappointed by the fact that there were no details around SkyWeaver. Poised to be operational in Q4 (now), but we haven’t had any updates. Bearing in mind that in the Q2 call, management confirmed it completed successful trials, it was strange not to have anything here.

In terms of financials, I’m concerned by how easily the increase in adjusted EBIDTA losses was communicated on the call. In Q2, Mr Laird stated that: “the second quarter would represent the peak in adjusted EBITDA losses”. To then proceed to have this increase by $13.4m QoQ isn’t reassuring.

SBC was insane again too, I expected a much larger decrease than what we had QoQ. The talent stacking is essential and has obviously helped propel the business forward, but this needs to decrease.

Margins have compressed and will continue to be compressed for the foreseeable, losses widened and hopefully should have peaked now, whilst adj EBITDA profitability has been pulled in once again.

For me, it’s a real mixed bag. And I think a lot of you may feel the same way looking at my timeline.

All in all. It’s not thesis breaking.

The revenue ramp is real, the demand/opportunity is real, the technology is fantastic and the system of systems approach is starting to bear fruit.

I’ve always backed the Core + Strategic M&A Growth Plan. From the start. But, as Eric said on the call, acquisitions need to moderate now.

Ondas have a complete company, you have all the tools you need to be successful, give or take a few exceptions to strengthen the portfolio.

Now you need to execute.

黄仁勋还会回来吗？我的答案是：短期内基本不可能。

他这次走得太干脆了。CEO、总裁、董事会席位一次全交，公司还专门设了史上第一个独立董事长，由做过$SNPS CEO的Harvey Jones担任。董事会这一套操作，就是要跟他切割干净，不是让他先出去避避风头。

再加上董事会请律所做的调查还没出结论，众议院听证、股东诉讼也都悬着。这些没落地之前，谁敢拉他回来，谁就是在给自己找麻烦。

有人会拿乔布斯举例。1985年他被赶出$AAPL，1997年又回来了。

可那中间隔了12年，而且是$AAPL快撑不下去了，才不得不请他回去。

$NVDA现在订单满满，没有"非他不可"的压力。真要等到黄仁勋回来，大概只有一种情况：新CEO做砸了，公司真出了大问题，调查又还了他清白。那是好几年以后的事。

那没有黄仁勋的$NVDA，谁来救？

现在临时坐镇的是CFO Colette Kress，跟了黄仁勋13年，华尔街很信任她。可她是管钱的，不是定方向的，市场普遍把她看成过渡人选。

内部呼声最高的是Ian Buck。CUDA就是他当年参与搞出来的，现在管数据中心和超算业务，在$NVDA干了快20年。截至今天早上，Polymarket给他的胜率是34%，Kalshi给到42%，两边都排第一。

问题是，Buck是顶级工程师，可黄仁勋干的不止是技术。跟$TSM谈产能，跟各国政府打交道，在台上把AI的故事讲给全世界听，这些Buck都没干过。

管全球销售的Jay Puri，联合创始人Chris Malachowsky，也都有人提。外部的名字里有$ARM的CEO Rene Haas，甚至还有$AMD的苏姿丰。可苏姿丰在$AMD干得好好的，$NVDA越乱她越占便宜，没理由跳槽。

最有意思的是库克。

Kalshi上一度有人押库克接任$NVDA，概率冲到过91%，后来又掉回10%左右。他现在是$AAPL的董事长，管全球供应链确实是世界第一。

可库克能来吗？我觉得很难。

一是他自己未必愿意。在$AAPL当董事长、功成名就，没必要跳进一个还在被调查、被听证的烂摊子。

二是就算来了，$NVDA缺的也不只是一个会管供应链的人。库克接手$AAPL的时候，产品方向是乔布斯定好的，iPhone已经是现成的。$NVDA现在缺的，正是那个定方向、敢豪赌的人。

说到底，黄仁勋一个人身上压了三个角色：总工程师、首席销售、首席讲故事的人。外面的人不管是谁，最多只能补上其中一块。

所以接下来$NVDA最可能的结果，是换成一个团队来扛：Buck这样的技术派管产品，Kress和Puri管钱和客户，董事长管治理。生意能守住，可那种一个人拍板、整个产业链跟着跑的速度，大概率回不来了。

对股价来说，这就是一段不确定期。新CEO人选出来之前，估值很难真正修复。

我的做法：不上杠杆，不赌财报，按价位分批。11/17看财报和管理层怎么讲，新CEO是谁出来了，再重新给它定价。

Melvin@MelvinInvests23h
I think bank stocks could be heading into some serious trouble and the bond market is one of the biggest reasons why.

The 10 year Treasury yield has now climbed above 5.2%, and that's creating a pretty interesting problem for banks. When Treasury yields rise, the value of existing bonds falls. That's a problem because banks hold enormous amounts of Treasury bonds and mortgage backed securities that were purchased when interest rates were much lower.

For example, imagine a bank bought billions of dollars worth of bonds paying 3% interest. Now investors can buy newly issued bonds paying over 5%. Those older bonds become less attractive, so their market value drops. And we're already seeing the damage. U.S. banks were sitting on nearly $330 billion in unrealized securities losses at the end of Q2 2026 but it gets worse.

Higher Treasury yields also mean banks have to compete harder to keep customer deposits. Why leave your money in a savings account paying very little when you can earn around 5% in government bonds or other non traditional banks that are paying much more ? That forces banks to offer higher deposit rates, increasing their costs. At the same time, higher borrowing costs can slow mortgage demand, hurt businesses, and eventually increase loan defaults. So banks could find themselves dealing with falling bond values, higher funding costs, and weaker lending demand all at once.

And we're already seeing investors react, just look at bank stocks. $JPM is down nearly 11%, $BAC is down almost 19%, $WFC is down 18%, and $C is down nearly 15%, even as Wall Street expects some of the biggest banks to report strong earnings.

Now, I'm not saying we're heading into another 2022 banking crisis. Banks are generally better positioned today, and rising rates can still benefit some lenders but I do think the bond market is flashing a warning sign that investors shouldn't ignore, especially when it comes to smaller regional banks. 尤其到了年底，基金经理还需要考虑全年业绩、持仓结构以及与基准指数之间的表现差距。如果$QQQ继续上涨，而部分机构因为过度谨慎明显跑输指数，就可能出现追赶基准的买盘。

另外还有空头。如果前期因为AI泡沫、高利率等理由建立了大量空头仓位，而股价反而持续突破，这些仓位可能被迫回补，进一步放大上涨幅度。

这就是典型的逼空行情形成条件：新增多头买盘、空头回补以及踏空资金追涨相互强化。

不过需要注意，市场上涨并不必然意味着发生逼空。真正判断逼空，还需要观察空头仓位、借券成本、期权Gamma和成交量。

我尤其关注$AVGO、$MU、$SNDK以及$LITE这些弹性较大的股票。如果未来盈利预期继续上修，同时出现放量突破，行情可能非常值得期待。

但我的观点始终没变，我宁愿错过一段上涨，也不会为了追赶行情随意增加杠杆。

真正折磨投资者的行情，有时候并不是暴跌，而是所有人都在等下跌，市场却始终不给机会。

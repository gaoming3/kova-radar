Personally, I think the vast majority of that CapEx will ultimately be funded through customer prepayments and GPU financing.

GPU-backed debt in particular is one of the more lender-friendly forms of financing, since it is supported by both the cash flows from the underlying customer contracts and the GPUs themselves.

So even in a more challenging financing environment, I don’t believe securing GPU-backed debt should pose a major challenge for $IREN. And if it does, the company has plenty of other non-dilutive sources of capital at its disposal.

In any case, Thursday’s earnings call made it clear that $IREN is better capitalized than ever and no longer has to rely almost entirely on equity-related sources of capital to fund its growth.

It’s also worth taking a moment to appreciate what all that dilution, which caused plenty of investor grief over the years, actually bought shareholders.

By the end of FY27 in June, management is guiding for the business to own roughly $10B of data center assets.

Those assets can now be borrowed against to help fund the next stage of growth, creating a much more capital-efficient financing flywheel as the infrastructure base expands.

The days of heavy dilution are truly coming to an end.

I wouldn’t be surprised to see $IREN begin buying back shares as early as the early 2030s.

我看到的是另一条线。稳定币的真正价值不在当下的 net interest income，而在 USDC 被嵌进多少条支付轨道、多少个交易所的结算层、多少个链上协议的默认计价单位。这些是 switching cost 极高的 structural 嵌入，一旦锁住就很难拔出来。利息是周期的，网络是结构的。

我在卖方还在纠结利差敏感度的时候就提示过这个错位：大家在给一只债券估值，但它正在变成一条清算网络。

降息真会杀死 Circle，还是只是把市场的注意力从利息逼回到它真正的护城河上？

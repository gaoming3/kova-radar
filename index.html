$161 TRILLION IN FIXED INCOME. WHY GRVT WANTS A SHARE OF THE YIELD MARKET

The next phase of onchain finance may be less about creating new token incentives and more about making existing financial cash flows accessible to everyday users.

@grvt_io is building toward that opportunity with Grvt Earn.

The scale is significant. Grvt cites a $161 trillion global fixed income market, where returns are generated through instruments such as bonds, credit strategies and other income producing assets.

Its upcoming Earn product targets yields of up to 11% APY, with returns expected to come from underlying financial activity, including interest, insurance-related premiums and invoice income.

The number deserves context.

At an illustrative 11% APY, a $1,000 balance would generate $110 over a year if that rate were sustained. A $10,000 balance would generate $1,100, before fees, taxes or other costs.

These are projections, not promised returns. Actual performance depends on the underlying strategies, their risks and the rates available over time.

$Grvt has also advertised a 3.5% base yield, making it important to distinguish the standard rate from the higher targeted investment opportunities.

The bigger opportunity is capital efficiency.

Grvt plans to introduce Yield as Margin, allowing eligible Earn balances to generate yield while supporting trading positions.

That could reduce the trade off between earning on idle capital and keeping funds available for market opportunities.

Grvt has also announced plans to build a $100 million position in Ondo’s USDY over a year, demonstrating its broader interest in tokenized Treasury backed yield.

Still, three questions matter before judging the opportunity:

How much of the target yield reaches users after costs?

What liquidity and counterparty risks come with the underlying strategies?

And can the platform sustain competitive returns across changing market conditions?

Grvt Earn’s proposition is straightforward: connect self custodial capital to established financial yield sources, then make that capital useful within the same trading ecosystem.

The execution, transparency and risk management will determine how compelling the model becomes.

ZERO TRADING FEES, DAILY FUNDING, 1,000+ MARKETS: GRVT’S NEXT MOVE IN ONCHAIN DERIVATIVES

The cost of a perpetual trade goes beyond the entry price.

Trading fees reduce returns with every execution. Funding payments accumulate while positions remain open. Limited market coverage can also force traders to split capital across platforms.

@grvt_io is targeting all three frictions with Stable Funding Perps, scheduled for November.

1. Zero trading fees across traditional markets

$GRVT plans to introduce perpetual contracts linked to stocks, foreign exchange, commodities and indices, with zero trading fees.

That could reduce one direct cost for active traders. Spreads, slippage, funding payments and other applicable costs still matter when calculating the actual cost of a position.

2. Funding charged once daily

Many crypto perpetual markets settle funding every one, four or eight hours, depending on the instrument and venue.

GRVT’s Stable Funding Perps are designed to charge funding once per day, with the rate intended to be more predictable and generally lower than competing venues.

For a trader holding a position over several days or weeks, knowing the funding terms before entry can make position-cost estimates easier.

The important distinction: fewer funding settlements do not automatically mean lower total funding. The rate itself determines the cost.

3. More than 1,000 markets by December

GRVT aims to list over 1,000 markets by the end of December 2026, expanding access to traditional assets through perpetual contracts.

The target matters because broader coverage could let traders manage exposure to different asset classes from one platform instead of relying exclusively on crypto markets.

The actual value will depend on market availability, liquidity and execution quality across those listings.

4. The infrastructure behind the experience

GRVT operates an onchain trading architecture with offchain order matching and cryptographic verification. The platform says its infrastructure is designed to combine fast execution with verifiable settlement.

That distinction is important for derivatives, where speed matters but so do risk controls, collateral handling and settlement integrity.

The bigger picture

GRVT is building toward a trading environment where access to traditional markets, predictable funding and lower execution costs sit within a self-custodial framework.

The numbers to watch after launch are effective trading costs, realized funding payments, spreads, market depth and how quickly new listings attract genuine liquidity.

Zero fees make a strong headline. Consistently competitive execution is what will determine whether the product delivers.

Who Loses If $GME Warrants Expire Worthless?

Everyone's watching the October 30 expiry and asking why Ryan Cohen isn't doing anything.

Wrong question.

The right question: who has the most to lose?

One month ago, GameStop completed a convertible note exchange.

Bondholders surrendered safe, interest-bearing debt for 55 million shares of GME equity at ~$18 VWAP.

These same bondholders also hold warrants from the October 2025 distribution, allocated on an as-converted basis.

Their decision to take equity over bonds wasn't charity.

It was a calculated bet on total return: shares PLUS warrants.

The warrant upside was baked into the conversion math.

If those warrants expire at zero:

- The risk-adjusted return on their conversion collapses

- A stock dip below $18 puts them underwater on the whole trade

- Cohen loses institutional credibility heading into the biggest acquisition bid of his career

Meanwhile, Cohen has been buying shares on the open market, just accumulation and silence.

He designed this instrument. He knows the bondholder math.

He knows the expiry date. He issued the warrants to those same bondholders.

He doesn’t need to print warrants ITM for retail alone.

He needs to for the holders he convinced to go from Arb trade to full long.

So either the architect of this entire capital structure is about to burn every institutional ally he needs for a $56 billion hostile bid for $EBAY.

Or something is coming.

英伟达在GTC柏林宣布CPO光引擎扩产，A股光模块龙头集体重挫，新易盛跌超11%，中际旭创跌超8%，天孚通信跌超7%。这两件事放在一起，不矛盾，但很扎心。

先看英伟达这份扩产名单到底给了谁。10月21日GTC柏林首日，英伟达宣布与六家供应商签署产能扩张协议，覆盖基板、先进封装、半导体测试和光引擎四个环节。名单是：安靠科技、揖斐电、京元电子、三星电机、矽品精密、欣兴电子。

CPO光引擎，扩产主体是矽品精密。英伟达与矽品计划在台湾扩大CPO光引擎制造产能，2027年中提升到目前的15倍，2028年底再翻倍到30倍，是这次扩产幅度最大的项目。光引擎负责光电信号转换，是CPO交换器的核心环节，背后用的是台积电的COUPE硅光子整合技术。

测试端，京元电子拿到11亿美元的产能预订预付款，用于在美国兴建新的测试设施和设备，目标2027年4月开始大规模量产。先进封装端，安靠负责扩产，到2028年底将中介层晶圆产能增加超过300%。基板端，揖斐电日本大野工厂扩产后目标产能达到当前的3倍，三星电机利用韩国现有产能供货并后续扩产，欣兴电子台湾KF基板2027年二季度开始贡献产能。英伟达整体基板产能目标三年内提升到2.5倍。

这份名单里，没有一家大陆厂商。

但名单之外，Lumentum和AAOI并没有消失，它们只是站的位置不同。

Lumentum不在名单上，因为它站在更上游。英伟达3月已经直接向Lumentum和Coherent各投资20亿美元，签下大额采购承诺、锁定未来产能使用权。在英伟达8月宣布量产的CPO交换机系统里，分工写得很清楚：Lumentum提供激光芯片，天孚通信做模块子组件组装，台积电做硅光制造，矽品完成封装。LITE不在GTC那份名单里，不是被排除，是它早已通过战略投资嵌在了更难被替换的上游位置。

AAOI走的是另一条路。它的核心战场在可插拔光模块，不在CPO。英伟达在GTC 2026上同时确认，可插拔光模块的需求跑道延长到了2027年，800G和1.6T的订单能见度依然存在。AAOI一季度已经开始发运800G收发器，拿到了超大规模客户金额逾2亿美元的1.6T首批量产订单，计划年底把月产能扩到65万只以上。它不在CPO扩产名单里，是因为它当前的位置在可插拔这条平行赛道上，而这条赛道在2026到2027年仍有明确的需求支撑。

回到A股这根阴线，它定价的逻辑其实很清楚。

大陆光模块龙头的主战场，目前仍然在可插拔模块的存量里。800G和1.6T的需求还在，中际旭创自己也回应过1.6T需求没有缩减。但英伟达这次CPO扩产的官宣主体里没有大陆厂商，市场就把这件事读成了一个信号：下一代的增量产能，可能不在他们手里。

资金已经在动了。中欧基金三季度把中际旭创的持股砍了35%以上，新易盛减了近28%。这不是赎回逼出来的被动卖出，是主动的策略再平衡。

所以今天这根阴线，本质上是同一个技术趋势在两个市场上给出了相反的定价。英伟达在买CPO的未来，A股在卖可插拔的现在。CPO的产业化节奏比市场预期快，这件事对整条链的需求是确认，但对不同位置的厂商，是重新分蛋糕。

后面要盯的，不是光模块还行不行这种笼统的问题，是很具体的一件事：大陆厂商在CPO的光引擎和激光器环节里，能不能挤进英伟达的供应链名单。挤进去了，今天跌的就是情绪坑；挤不进去，分化的定价才刚开始。

AAOI走的是另一条路。它的核心战场在可插拔光模块，不在CPO。英伟达在GTC 2026上同时确认，可插拔光模块的需求跑道延长到了2027年，800G和1.6T的订单能见度依然存在。AAOI一季度已经开始发运800G收发器，拿到了超大规模客户金额逾2亿美元的1.6T首批量产订单，计划年底把月产能扩到65万只以上。它不在CPO扩产名单里，是因为它当前的位置在可插拔这条平行赛道上，而这条赛道在2026到2027年仍有明确的需求支撑。

回到A股这根阴线，它定价的逻辑其实很清楚。

大陆光模块龙头的主战场，目前仍然在可插拔模块的存量里。800G和1.6T的需求还在，中际旭创自己也回应过1.6T需求没有缩减。但英伟达这次CPO扩产的官宣主体里没有大陆厂商，市场就把这件事读成了一个信号：下一代的增量产能，可能不在他们手里。

资金已经在动了。中欧基金三季度把中际旭创的持股砍了35%以上，新易盛减了近28%。这不是赎回逼出来的被动卖出，是主动的策略再平衡。

所以今天这根阴线，本质上是同一个技术趋势在两个市场上给出了相反的定价。英伟达在买CPO的未来，A股在卖可插拔的现在。CPO的产业化节奏比市场预期快，这件事对整条链的需求是确认，但对不同位置的厂商，是重新分蛋糕。

后面要盯的，不是光模块还行不行这种笼统的问题，是很具体的一件事：大陆厂商在CPO的光引擎和激光器环节里，能不能挤进英伟达的供应链名单。挤进去了，今天跌的就是情绪坑；挤不进去，分化的定价才刚开始。

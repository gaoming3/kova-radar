$161 TRILLION IN FIXED INCOME. WHY GRVT WANTS A SHARE OF THE YIELD MARKET

The next phase of onchain finance may be less about creating new token incentives and more about making existing financial cash flows accessible to everyday users.

@grvt_io is building toward that opportunity with Grvt Earn.

The scale is significant. Grvt cites a $161 trillion global fixed income market, where returns are generated through instruments such as bonds, credit strategies and other income producing assets.

Its upcoming Earn product targets yields of up to 11% APY, with returns expected to come from underlying financial activity, including interest, insurance-related premiums and invoice income.

The number deserves context.

At an illustrative 11% APY, a $1,000 balance would generate $110 over a year if that rate were sustained. A $10,000 balance would generate $1,100, before fees, taxes or other costs.

These are projections, not promised returns. Actual performance depends on the underlying strategies, their risks and the rates available over time.

$Grvt has also advertised a 3.5% base yield, making it important to distinguish the standard rate from the higher targeted investment opportunities.

The bigger opportunity is capital efficiency.

Grvt plans to introduce Yield as Margin, allowing eligible Earn balances to generate yield while supporting trading positions.

That could reduce the trade off between earning on idle capital and keeping funds available for market opportunities.

Grvt has also announced plans to build a $100 million position in Ondo’s USDY over a year, demonstrating its broader interest in tokenized Treasury backed yield.

Still, three questions matter before judging the opportunity:

How much of the target yield reaches users after costs?

What liquidity and counterparty risks come with the underlying strategies?

And can the platform sustain competitive returns across changing market conditions?

Grvt Earn’s proposition is straightforward: connect self custodial capital to established financial yield sources, then make that capital useful within the same trading ecosystem.

The execution, transparency and risk management will determine how compelling the model becomes.

I love selling options.

Someone is willing to pay me $970 to buy $GOOGL at $300 by January.

Google is currently trading at $323.

It's called a cash-secured put, and here's how it works:

You sell the $300 put expiring January 15, 2027 and collect $970 upfront. You set aside $30,000 in case you have to buy 100 shares at $300.

From here, one of two things happens:

1. $GOOGL stays above $300 through January 15. The put expires worthless and you keep the $970. That's a 3.2% return on $30,000 in 84 days, about 14% annualized.

2. $GOOGL drops below $300. You buy 100 shares at $300, but the $970 lowers your real cost to $290.30 per share. That's about 10% below where Google trades today.

Alphabet reports earnings on Oct 28, which falls inside this window. That's part of why the premium is this rich.

If Google falls well below $290.30, you're buying shares at a loss compared to the market price. Only sell puts on stocks you'd be happy to own.

Not financial advice. I share these for education.

- Google Cloud：云业务能否继续保持

如果这次云业务继续超预期，说明 AI 需求仍然强劲，对谷歌肯定是个加分项。

但我也有一个顾虑，谷歌现在为了 AI 投入了大量资金，数据中心、芯片和算力建设都需要真金白银。

云业务增长再快，如果资本开支持续增加，却没能带来相应的利润和现金流，市场可能就要重新评估这些投入了。

所以这次财报，我主要看三个指标📊：

Google Cloud 增速、AI 资本开支，以及搜索广告收入。

综合来看，我个人还是偏多，主要看好谷歌在云计算和 AI 领域的长期竞争力。

不过财报好不代表股价一定涨，最终还得看实际数据能否超过市场预期。

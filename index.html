The White House crypto meeting happened today, and now that we know what actually came out of the room, it’s worth separating that from everything people were predicting beforehand.

President Trump sat down with Brad Garlinghouse, Brian Armstrong, Vlad Tenev, the Winklevoss twins, Arjun Sethi, Sergey Nazarov and other major crypto executives. SEC Chair Paul Atkins and CFTC Chair Michael Selig were there too, along with leadership from traditional finance including Nasdaq and Intercontinental Exchange/NYSE.

Trump’s biggest push was the CLARITY Act. He called on Congress to pass a fair version of the market structure bill and described it as “very, very powerful” legislation.

CLARITY is supposed to establish clearer federal rules for digital assets and define how oversight is divided between regulators instead of leaving the industry guessing which rules apply.

CFTC Chair Michael Selig reinforced that direction, saying the era of regulation by enforcement and de-banking is over and that the CFTC is prepared to implement CLARITY if it becomes law.

Trump also discussed the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and the separate Digital Asset Stockpile. Bitcoin gets the reserve designation. Other digital assets fall under the stockpile framework. When Trump was asked whether the United States could accumulate sizable amounts of Bitcoin or other crypto beyond what it already controls, his response was, “Certainly, it’s been talked about.”

That isn’t an announcement that the government is buying crypto. It isn’t confirmation that the government is buying $XRP either. It means the subject has been discussed. Until there’s an actual policy, authorization or transaction, that’s as far as we can take it.

For $XRP specifically, Brad Garlinghouse being in that room is significant enough without inventing anything around it. Ripple had a seat at the table with the President, SEC, CFTC, major crypto companies and some of the biggest names in traditional financial markets.

Nothing announced today made $XRP part of the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve. Nothing announced a government $XRP purchase. Nothing announced that DTCC, Nasdaq, NYSE or the federal government selected XRPL for settlement.

What we actually got was something much broader. The President publicly pushed Congress for crypto market structure legislation while the people who’ll build, regulate and operate inside that framework were sitting in the room with him.

That’s what happened today.

No need to make it bigger than it was. It was already a pretty fucking important meeting.

美股七巨頭 $MAGS 或者市值前十大的股票，約佔美股市場總市值的40%。自2014年起，這些股票開始主導市場，直到最近一年左右，只要七巨頭遭遇拋售，便會拖累整個大盤下跌，因為這些股票的體量龐大對大盤指數影響巨大。

多年來，此種情況一再發生。然而，今年以來情況發生了顯著變化。市場出現了被稱為「牛市廣度擴大」（bullish broadening）的現象。七巨頭下跌、AI相關股票下跌，而標普500指數 $SPY 整體表現卻相對穩定。

標普等權重指數 $RSP 在今年的表現，甚至超越了七巨頭整整13個百分點，這表明「493們」在積極地上漲——與過去多年的現象相比，這是一個根本的改變。

這對主動選股的投資者非常有利的，對於那些希望避開 AI 股的投資者來說也是好消息，因為，可選的標的更多了。

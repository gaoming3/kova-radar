Lots of retail investors seem to have PTSD from the last 3 earnings calls, where the stock either dumped right away or quickly faded after an initial pop.

In fact, the last earnings call where the stock held its post-earnings gains and continued rallying in the days and weeks afterwards was exactly 1 year ago.

Will history repeat?

This year has certainly been a frustrating one as an $IREN investor. If we still want to see some strong gains into year-end, now is definitely the time to kick-start a new rally.

As for my expectations for today’s results, I honestly care less about the financials themselves.

We already know $IREN is in the midst of an extraordinary ramp, so dwelling on the exact figures of what they made between April and June this year is a bit like looking in the rear-view mirror while driving 150 miles per hour on the Autobahn.

As a long-term-oriented investor, I care less about that kind of stuff at this stage of the business.

But if anyone has a good pulse on where the numbers are going to land, it’s my good friend @_Sgr_A_Star, who actually thinks the odds are favorable for $IREN to slightly beat revenue expectations while probably falling short on earnings expectations due to Bitcoin miner impairments from selling ASICs amid the company’s transition toward AI cloud.

In any case, I’ll be much more focused on news that gives us additional color on the bigger picture.

That includes any long-term guidance, the evolving customer mix, insights into contract structures and terms, how specific capacity cohorts are being allocated across different customer profiles (SW1 next year, Childress air-cooled, H5-H6, etc.), updates on financing strategy and asset-backed financing for the Horizon modules, and of course any new deal or partnership announcements.

If we do get a new deal announcement, I believe the highest probability is around Childress’s remaining 50 MW of air-cooled capacity that is still expected to be contracted this year as part of 2026 year-end guidance, as well as the remaining 190 MW of air-cooled capacity slated for 2027.

I think it’s very possible that whoever takes those initial 50 MW will also take some or all of the additional 190 MW coming online next year.

Unless that capacity is instead being targeted toward a broader mix of enterprise customers. But even if that’s the case, I’d expect management to give us more guidance on that today.

You already know I’ll be listening to $IREN's call like a hawk and working on a breakdown over the coming days, which is where I believe I do some of my best work.

Fingers crossed. Cheers guys! 🤞

$NVDA 财报最被忽视的细节: $SPCX 可能才是隐藏的大赢家。

英伟达说，合作的Neocloud到2026年底将拥有8GW已安装容量。

但公开数字暂时对不上：

CoreWeave年底超过1.85GW；
Nebius年底约0.8至1.0GW。
两家最大的上市纯Neocloud，加起来只有约2.7至2.9GW。

剩下约5GW去了哪里？

这5GW不能直接全部算给SpaceX，里面还有Nscale、Crusoe、Lambda、Fluidstack、IREN等运营商。

但SpaceX很可能藏在其中，而且规模不小。

三条官方数据已经对上：

SpaceX二季度AI名义算力达到1.4GW，一年前只有0.4GW；

同季签下141亿美元云服务合同，带来16亿美元新增AI基础设施收入；

NVDA在财报前两天宣布，SpaceXAI将使用Vera Rubin继续扩张至GW级算力，并准备把同一架构送入轨道。

这意味着SpaceX正在发生一个重要变化：

过去，它是花钱购买GPU、训练Grok的客户。

现在，它开始把这些GPU出租给Google、Anthropic等外部客户，变成AI算力运营商。

而黄仁勋在电话会上给出了一个惊人的数字：

Hopper每GW对应约180亿美元英伟达平台收入；
Grace Blackwell约250亿美元；
Vera Rubin约400亿美元。

注意：这400亿美元不是SpaceX的收入。
但它说明，SpaceX账上那1.4GW已经不是一组抽象的机房数字，而是一批极其昂贵、同时可能产生长期租金收入的基础设施资产。
所以，NVDA财报真正替SPCX验证的不是“GPU还能卖多少”，而是：

SpaceX豪赌的算力，可能真的有人租，而且租金正在变成收入。

我现在的判断：

英伟达没有证明那5GW属于SpaceX，但SpaceX很可能是这份NVDA财报最被忽视的映射受益者。

关注我，我会继续追踪这5GW最后到底属于谁。
非投资建议。

在 @Tangent_fi 借 $USG ，HEC和LEC其实很好分。

我看了下两套规则，你可能会停留在HEC的0%上，让人误会这笔钱白借免费。

HEC更适合能持续产出流动奖励的抵押品，比如Curve LP。市价在门槛之上时不收现金利息，但会按比例扣抵押品的奖励，价格跌得越多扣得越多；跌破门槛后奖励直接扣满100%，同时开始计息，债务才会往上滚。

LEC是另一套逻辑，通常对应低奖励甚至零流动奖励的资产，比如Pendle PT。开仓就开始计息，债务会慢慢累积；如果抵押品本身还有流动奖励，按当前规则是97.5%归借款人，协议收2.5%。

这么放一起对比就很清楚了。HEC的成本更多是拿奖励，换来一段时间债务不增长；LEC是把大部分奖励留在手里，但利息从开仓那一刻就在算。选哪边，主要看你更在意哪种成本。

手里要是高奖励的Curve LP，又不想看着债务一直涨，选HEC会更顺手。要是抵押品奖励本来就不高，或者更想把奖励攥在手里、能接受利息慢慢累积，那LEC更合适。实际测算的时候，奖励、利息和清算空间都得放在一起考量。

还有个很容易被忽略的细节，官方写的是HEC和LEC的市场类型可以经DAO投票调整，但这不代表现有仓位随时能一键切换。具体抵押率、价格门槛、利率曲线和额度，还是要以App内当前市场的实际参数为准。

真打算借USG的话，先点开自己抵押品对应的市场，把利率、门槛和清算线都过一遍。HEC的0%是有前提条件的，LEC从开仓就开始计息，别被页面上数字带偏了。
